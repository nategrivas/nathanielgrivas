What the data shows

  • Mortgage rates rose this week and sit about a full point above where they were a year ago.

How it is measured

Sources and method

Listings: Zillow, active for-sale listings (houses, condos and townhomes), pulled November 27, 2026, counted by region across the 40 neighborhoods on this site. "Reduced" counts active listings Zillow flags as price reduced.

Mortgage rates: Freddie Mac PMMS, latest weekly survey as of 2026-11-19.

County prices and sales: C.A.R., as reported by NBC Los Angeles, October 2026, existing single-family homes.

Region counts use public listing data, so they can differ from MLS totals. Nathaniel can run any single neighborhood, pocket or price band from the MLS on request.
