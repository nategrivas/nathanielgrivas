The headline · November 2026

Nithya Raman is elected mayor of Los Angeles

By Nathaniel Grivas, from public reporting · November 5, 2026

City Councilmember Nithya Raman defeated incumbent Mayor Karen Bass in the Nov. 3 runoff. The Associated Press had Raman at 52.1% to Bass's 47.9% as of Nov. 5, when NBC News projected the win. Bass is the first incumbent LA mayor to lose a re-election bid since 2005. Raman takes office on Dec. 14, 2026.

Sources: NBC Los Angeles, Daily Bruin, Ballotpedia

The week in LA · Nov. 20 to 26, 2026

Courts

Jury convicts Jonathan Rinderknecht of starting the fire that became the Palisades Fire

November 20, 2026 · Pacific Palisades

A federal jury in Los Angeles found Rinderknecht guilty on two arson counts on Friday, Nov. 20, after a two-week retrial. His first trial ended in a mistrial in June with jurors split 10 to 2 for acquittal. Prosecutors say the New Year's Day 2025 Lachman Fire smoldered underground and reignited as the Palisades Fire on Jan. 7, 2025, which killed 12 people and destroyed thousands of homes.

Sources: NBC Los Angeles, ABC News, KTVU

Real estate

Netflix buys the Radford Studio Center lot in Studio City for $380 million

November 24, 2026 · Studio City

The 55-acre lot drew no bids at a foreclosure auction on Nov. 24 and went back to its Goldman Sachs-led lenders, and Netflix announced its purchase shortly after. The price is about a fifth of the $1.85 billion Hackman Capital paid in 2021. The lot will keep operating as a working studio.

Sources: ABC7, The Real Deal

City Hall

LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell says the mayor-elect hasn't decided whether he stays through the Olympics

November 25, 2026

Speaking on Nov. 25, McDonnell said Mayor-elect Nithya Raman has not yet decided whether he will remain chief through the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games. Raman takes office on Dec. 14.

Sources: LAist

TodayFriday, November 27, 2026

Low 80s today before back-to-back storms next week, the Hollywood Christmas Parade closes streets Sunday, USC plays UCLA at the Rose Bowl, and LAUSD switches off Google's AI summaries.

Weather

Low 80s for Black Friday, then back-to-back storms next week

Friday brought sunshine and highs around 83 in the basin and the valleys, with gusty winds through the canyons. The weekend cools a bit. A light storm is expected Monday, then a stronger one Tuesday into Wednesday that could bring 2 to 4 inches of rain depending on location, plus mountain snow. Clear gutters and check your drains before Tuesday.

Sources: ABC7

Beaches

King tides ease and the Manhattan Beach Pier is open again

Malibu, Santa Monica, Venice, Playa del Rey

The Manhattan Beach Pier reopened Thursday afternoon after high surf forced it closed. A coastal flood advisory for the LA County coast ran until noon Friday, and a high surf advisory stays in effect until 9 p.m. Saturday, so expect strong currents if you head to the sand this weekend.

Sources: KTLA, ABC7

Things to do

The Hollywood Christmas Parade rolls Sunday at 6 p.m.

Hollywood

The 94th parade runs a loop of Hollywood and Sunset boulevards on Sunday, Nov. 29, with pre-show entertainment at 5:30 p.m. Hollywood Boulevard closes at La Brea Avenue starting at 6 a.m., and Sunset closes between La Brea and Highland in the afternoon. Reserved grandstand seats are sold out, but curbside viewing is free along much of the route east of Highland. Metro is the easy way in.

Sources: Beverly Press, Our Los Angeles, Hollywood Christmas Parade

Sports

USC and UCLA close the regular season at the Rose Bowl Saturday

UCLA hosts crosstown rival USC in Pasadena on Saturday, Nov. 28, to end the 2026 regular season. USC won last year's meeting 29-10 at the Coliseum.

Sources: Sports Illustrated, theScore

Things to do

Last weekend for the LA Auto Show downtown

The LA Auto Show wraps up its 10-day run at the Convention Center on Sunday, Nov. 29. Expect new hybrids and EVs, test drives and a classic car section.

Sources: ABC7, NBC Los Angeles

Schools

LAUSD turns off Google's AI summaries on student devices

Los Angeles Unified has disabled Google's AI Overview in searches on school-issued devices, calling the step temporary while it reviews student safeguards. The move comes as Google has said schools that block its AI features could lose access to search, and some districts are weighing other search providers.

Sources: ABC7, PBS NewsHour

Real estate

A new Brentwood estate sells for $39 million

Brentwood

A newly built home at 401 N. Tigertail Road in Brentwood sold for $39 million, The Real Deal reported this week. It first shopped off market at $75 million and was later listed at $65 million.

Sources: The Real Deal, Listing record

Shopping

Black Friday crowds start the night before

Shoppers headed to Citadel Outlets in Commerce straight from Thanksgiving dinner for its overnight sale, with discounts of up to 70% and the doors open until 11 p.m. Friday. Saturday is Small Business Saturday, a good day to shop the neighborhood stores near you.

Sources: KTLA

Also this monthNovember 2026

Election

Xavier Becerra is elected governor of California

November 3, 2026

Democrat Xavier Becerra defeated Republican Steve Hilton on Nov. 3 to succeed term-limited Gov. Gavin Newsom. The AP called the race shortly after polls closed. Becerra will be the first Latino elected governor of California.

Sources: KQED, OPB / Associated Press

Election

Sheriff Robert Luna wins a second term over Alex Villanueva

November 3, 2026

LA County Sheriff Robert Luna defeated his predecessor Alex Villanueva in the Nov. 3 runoff, a rematch of their 2022 race.

Sources: FOX 11, Los Angeles Magazine

Real estate

Measure TE, a Measure ULA break for Palisades Fire homes, is on track to pass

November 15, 2026 · Pacific Palisades

Mid-November counts had every Los Angeles city measure on the ballot passing, including Measure TE. It gives a one-time exemption from the Measure ULA transfer tax to homes damaged or destroyed in the Palisades Fire, if the owner of record on Jan. 7, 2025 sells by Jan. 6, 2030. Results become official when the county certifies them.

Sources: Daily Bruin, LAist voter guide
