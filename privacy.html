Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This site is run by Nathaniel Grivas, REALTOR® (DRE #02410660), with The Brodsky Group at Compass, 9454 Wilshire Blvd, Ste 100, Beverly Hills, CA 90212. Questions: nate.grivas@compass.com or 310-269-5541.

What I collect

What you give me. Your name, email, phone, the answers you choose or type in the search questions, a property address, and any message you send.

How you use the site. The pages you view and for how long, the buttons you tap (for example Call, Text, a listing or a neighborhood), the website or ad that brought you here, and your device type, browser, screen width, language and time zone. Your browser keeps a random visitor ID and this visit's activity in local storage so repeat visits can be connected.

When it is sent to me

Your activity is sent to me by email when you send a form, when you tap Call, Text or Email, and when you leave after starting a search or showing interest without reaching out. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, I don't send activity alerts. Forms you send still include your activity.

How I use it

To reply to you, set up home searches and listing alerts, follow up about buying, selling, renting, investing or building, and improve this site. Your contact details and search may be added to my client records at Compass.

Who it is shared with

I don't sell your information. It is shared with the services that run this site and deliver messages (Vercel for hosting, FormSubmit for form email), with Compass and The Brodsky Group to provide real estate services, and when the law requires it. Pages also load fonts and satellite maps from Google, neighborhood photos from Unsplash, aerial imagery from the U.S. Geological Survey, and a listing video from Vimeo when you scroll to it. These services receive standard web request data.

Calls and texts

If you send a form, you agree I can contact you by call, text or email about real estate. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

Email me to see, correct or delete what I have about you. You can also clear this site's storage in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site is not meant for anyone under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their information.
