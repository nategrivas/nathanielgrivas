## Neighborhood guides

- [Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu): Twenty-one miles of coast, its own city, and septic tanks.
- [Marina del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/marina-del-rey): Boats, high-rises and the bike path, run by the county.
- [Pacific Palisades](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/pacific-palisades): A beach town in the hills, rebuilding after the January 2025 fire.
- [Playa Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/playa-vista): A planned neighborhood with a park every few blocks.
- [Playa del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/playa-del-rey): The quiet beach town most of LA forgets about.
- [Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica): The beach city with its own rules, its own schools and a train to Downtown.
- [Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice): Abbot Kinney, the canals and the boardwalk, inside the City of LA.
- [Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/brentwood): Big lots, big trees, and the San Vicente coral trees.
- [Century City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/century-city): High-rise living on the old 20th Century Fox backlot.
- [Cheviot Hills & Rancho Park](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/cheviot-hills): Curving streets next to the best public park on the Westside.
- [Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/culver-city): Its own city, its own schools, and a downtown that works.
- [Mar Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/mar-vista): Westside residential streets and mid-century homes, minutes from the beach.
- [Palms](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/palms): Dense, central and close to everything. The Westside's renter neighborhood.
- [Pico-Robertson & Beverlywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/pico-robertson): Walkable, next to Beverly Hills, anchored by Pico Boulevard.
- [West LA & Sawtelle](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-la): Sawtelle Japantown and the Westside's best casual food.
- [Westchester](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/westchester): Post-war houses, real yards, and LMU on the bluff.
- [Westwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/westwood): UCLA, Westwood Village and the Wilshire Corridor towers.
- [Bel Air](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/bel-air): Gated entrances, estate lots and the biggest houses in LA.
- [Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills): Its own city, its own schools, its own police, and the Flats.
- [Beverly Hills Post Office](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/bhpo): A 90210 address in the City of LA. Canyons, views and privacy.
- [Hollywood Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/hollywood-hills): City views five minutes from Sunset.
- [Beverly Grove](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-grove): Third Street, the Beverly Center and Spanish duplexes on every block.
- [Fairfax District](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/fairfax): The Original Farmers Market, The Grove and blocks of Spanish duplexes.
- [Hancock Park & Larchmont](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/hancock-park): Old LA mansions, Larchmont Village and the Wilshire Country Club.
- [Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/hollywood): Studios, nightlife and the B Line. Live a few blocks off the boulevard.
- [Miracle Mile](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/miracle-mile): Museum Row on Wilshire and the subway underneath it.
- [West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-hollywood): The Sunset Strip, Santa Monica Boulevard and the Design District in under two square miles.
- [East Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/east-hollywood): Thai Town, Little Armenia and Barnsdall Park, right on the B Line.
- [Echo Park](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/echo-park): The lake, Dodger Stadium and hillside homes close to Downtown.
- [Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/los-feliz): Griffith Park as a backyard and Vermont Avenue for dinner.
- [Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/silver-lake): The reservoir loop, Sunset Junction and modernist houses on the hills.
- [Encino](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/encino): Big lots south of Ventura, condos north of it, and the Sepulveda Basin.
- [North Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/north-hollywood): The NoHo Arts District and the end of the B Line.
- [Reseda](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/reseda): Big flat lots and some of the most attainable houses in the City of LA.
- [Sherman Oaks](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/sherman-oaks): Ventura Boulevard, the Galleria and the 405/101 interchange.
- [Studio City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/studio-city): The Valley's most walkable stretch of Ventura Boulevard.
- [Tarzana](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/tarzana): Named for Tarzan, with hillside homes and room to spread out.
- [Valley Village](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/valley-village): Quiet streets between Studio City and NoHo.
- [Van Nuys](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/van-nuys): Entry-level houses, real lots and room for an ADU.
- [Woodland Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/woodland-hills): Warner Center, Topanga trails and the Valley's west end.
