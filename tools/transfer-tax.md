## How each city charges

- **City of Los Angeles:** 0.45% city tax on every sale, plus Measure ULA on the full price: 4% above $5.4M and 5.5% at $10.9M and up for sales closing July 1, 2026 or later. The thresholds adjust every July 1.
- **Santa Monica:** one city rate on the full price: 0.3% under $5M, 0.6% from $5M, 5.6% at $8M and up.
- **Culver City:** Measure RE brackets that work like income tax, each rate applying only to the slice of price inside it: 0.45% up to $1.5M, 1.5% to $3M, 3% to $10M, 4% above. The city can adjust the bracket lines for inflation, so confirm with Culver City.
- **Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Malibu and unincorporated areas like Marina del Rey:** no city transfer tax. Only the county's 0.11%.
- **Everyone:** LA County charges $1.10 per $1,000 (0.11%).

Who pays is negotiable. In LA the seller customarily pays transfer taxes, but it's a term in the contract. This calculator estimates taxes only. It skips exemptions, escrow, title and commission. Read more in [Measure ULA in 2026](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/measure-ula-mansion-tax-2026).
