LA isn't one city

Transfer taxes, rent control and schools change at every city line. Here's the map.

Los Angeles County has 88 cities, and the Westside alone has five of them sitting next to each other. Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Culver City, West Hollywood and Malibu each set their own transfer taxes, rent control and schools. Brentwood, Bel Air, Venice, Pacific Palisades and the Valley are all inside the City of LA. Marina del Rey belongs to no city at all.

That's how two homes a block apart can owe very different taxes at sale, follow different rent rules and feed into different school districts. A few that catch people:

  • 90210 isn't always Beverly Hills. The hills above it, Beverly Hills Post Office, use 90210 but are in the City of LA. See BHPO.
  • Measure ULA stops at the city line. A $6M sale in Brentwood pays it. The same sale in Santa Monica pays Santa Monica's tier instead.
  • Culver City taxes by bracket. Only the slice of price inside each bracket pays that bracket's rate.

To check an exact address, LA's ZIMAS shows whether a parcel is in the City of LA. The county's assessor portal shows the rest.
