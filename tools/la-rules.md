# LA isn't one city

Transfer taxes, rent control and schools change at every city line. Here's the map.

Los Angeles County has 88 cities, and the Westside alone has five of them sitting next to each other. Beverly Hills, Santa Monica, Culver City, West Hollywood and Malibu each set their own transfer taxes, rent control and schools. Brentwood, Bel Air, Venice, Pacific Palisades and the Valley are all inside the City of LA. Marina del Rey belongs to no city at all.

That's how two homes a block apart can owe very different taxes at sale, follow different rent rules and feed into different school districts. A few that catch people:

- **90210 isn't always Beverly Hills.** The hills above it, Beverly Hills Post Office, use 90210 but are in the City of LA. [See BHPO](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/bhpo).
- **Measure ULA stops at the city line.** A $6M sale in Brentwood pays it. The same sale in Santa Monica pays Santa Monica's tier instead.
- **Culver City taxes by bracket.** Only the slice of price inside each bracket pays that bracket's rate.

To check an exact address, LA's [ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org/) shows whether a parcel is in the City of LA. The county's assessor portal shows the rest.

### City of Los Angeles

- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)

### City of Santa Monica

- **Transfer tax:** City tax on the full price: 0.3% under $5M, 0.6% from $5M, 5.6% at $8M and up, plus 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** No. Santa Monica has its own top tier instead (5.6% at $8M+)
- **Rent control:** Santa Monica rent control covers most apartments built before April 10, 1979
- **Public schools:** Santa Monica-Malibu Unified (SMMUSD)

### City of Beverly Hills

- **Transfer tax:** 0.11% county only
- **Measure ULA:** No
- **Rent control:** Beverly Hills rent stabilization covers most multi-family rentals
- **Public schools:** Beverly Hills Unified (BHUSD)

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills)

### City of Culver City

- **Transfer tax:** Measure RE brackets from 0.45% up to 4% on the top slice, plus 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** No. Culver City has its own bracketed tax (Measure RE)
- **Rent control:** Culver City rent control covers most apartments built before February 1, 1995
- **Public schools:** Culver City Unified (CCUSD)

### City of West Hollywood

- **Transfer tax:** 0.11% county only
- **Measure ULA:** No
- **Rent control:** West Hollywood rent stabilization covers most apartments built before July 1, 1979
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-hollywood)

### City of Malibu

- **Transfer tax:** 0.11% county only
- **Measure ULA:** No
- **Rent control:** No local rent control. California's statewide cap (AB 1482) covers eligible rentals
- **Public schools:** Santa Monica-Malibu Unified, with a separate Malibu Unified in the works

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu)

### Unincorporated LA County

- **Transfer tax:** 0.11% county only
- **Measure ULA:** No
- **Rent control:** LA County's rent stabilization ordinance covers most apartments built before February 1, 1995
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
