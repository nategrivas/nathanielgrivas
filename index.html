Meet Nate

Nate Grivas

REALTOR® · DRE #02410660 · The Brodsky Group at Compass

Raised between New York and Seattle before relocating to Los Angeles, Nate represents buyers, sellers, landlords and developers across West Los Angeles, Beverly Hills and the San Fernando Valley.

His work covers pricing and listing prep, competitive offers, leasing, new construction, teardown and lot analysis, ADU feasibility and off-market sourcing. He studied Marketing and Business Law at Loyola Marymount University.
