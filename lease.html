Leasing questions

What do landlords in Los Angeles usually ask for?

A rental application, photo ID, proof of income (many landlords look for income around three times the rent), a credit report and references from past landlords. Having them ready makes your application stronger when several people apply for the same place.

Is the place rent controlled?

It depends on the city and the building. In the City of Los Angeles, most apartments built before October 1978 fall under the Rent Stabilization Ordinance, and California's statewide cap covers many other rentals. Santa Monica, West Hollywood, Culver City and Beverly Hills have their own rules. Nathaniel can check a specific address.

Can Nathaniel lease out my property?

Yes. He prices the rent from recent leases nearby, markets the home, screens applicants, and handles the lease and move-in. He leased 1127 Glenville Dr in Pico-Robertson, representing both the landlord and the tenant.
