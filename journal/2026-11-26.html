LA News · Thursday, November 26, 2026

King tides, the Netflix lot deal and a busy holiday weekend

Thanksgiving in LA: record high tides along the coast, trains halted south of Irvine, Netflix buys a historic Studio City lot, and what's on this weekend.

By Nathaniel Grivas, REALTOR® · Written in plain English with links to the original reporting

Today in LA

Weather

Record king tides and big surf hit the coast on Thanksgiving morning

An extreme high tide of about 8 feet arrived Thursday morning, with a coastal flood advisory for the Malibu coast and LA County beaches through noon and a high surf warning until 9 p.m. Forecasters expect waves of roughly 6 to 11 feet with strong rip currents, and the LA Harbor tied its all-time high-water mark. If you're heading to the beach this weekend, check tide times before parking in beach lots and stay off the rocks and jetties.

Neighborhoods: Malibu, Santa Monica, Venice, Marina del Rey, Playa del Rey · Sources: ABC7 live updates, Yahoo News / ABC7

Transit

Amtrak halts Pacific Surfliner trains between Irvine and Oceanside

High-tide washout damaged track near San Clemente, so Amtrak suspended Pacific Surfliner service between Irvine and Oceanside for the day. Taking the train to San Diego this weekend? Check pacificsurfliner.com before you leave, since more king tides are expected in December and January.

Sources: ABC7, Edhat

Real estate

Netflix buys the Radford Studio Center lot in Studio City for $380 million

The 55-acre, 22-stage lot where Seinfeld and Gunsmoke were filmed drew no bids at Tuesday's foreclosure auction in Pomona, so it went back to the Goldman Sachs-led lenders, and Netflix announced its purchase about an hour later. The price is about a fifth of the $1.85 billion Hackman Capital paid in 2021. ABC7 reports Netflix paid the city's Measure ULA transfer tax on the deal, and the lot will keep running as a working studio.

Neighborhoods: Studio City · Sources: ABC7, The Real Deal, Bloomberg

Weather

A historic El Niño winter is shaping up

UC climate scientist Daniel Swain's November update says this El Niño is already among the strongest on record for this time of year and still strengthening, which raises the odds of heavy rain and flooding in Southern California. The biggest local impacts usually arrive after the event peaks, so the wettest stretch could come after the new year. If you own a home, clear gutters and drains now and check drainage on hillside lots.

Sources: UC ANR El Niño update (PDF), FOX 11

Things to do

A packed holiday weekend: the Hollywood Christmas Parade, the Auto Show and holiday concerts

The Hollywood Christmas Parade returns Sunday, Nov. 29, and the LA Auto Show runs downtown through the same day. Walt Disney Concert Hall kicks off its holiday series this weekend with Home Alone and Elf screened with a live orchestra, and the Smorgasburg holiday market is on the calendar too.

Neighborhoods: Hollywood · Sources: Resident, We Like LA

Food

Two downtown classics are serving again: Cole's and the Original Pantry

Cole's, the French dip institution on 6th Street, has reopened with a more polished dining room, and the century-old Original Pantry Cafe is back serving breakfast, lunch and dinner under nonprofit management.

Sources: The Infatuation

Housing

LA rents kept cooling this fall

The median asking rent in the City of LA slipped 3.3% from a year earlier to $2,708 in the third quarter of 2026, part of a slow multi-year easing in housing costs across LA County.

Sources: Edhat
