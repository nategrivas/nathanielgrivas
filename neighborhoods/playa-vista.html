What it’s like

Everything is new and close: Runway for shopping and dinner, Central Park with a pool and courts, dozens of pocket parks, and the Ballona Wetlands next door. Very stroller friendly.

Playa Vista was built from scratch starting in the early 2000s on the former Hughes Aircraft site, where Howard Hughes built the Spruce Goose. The hangar is now office space, and tech campuses fill the area.

Living here

The homes, the rules and who it fits

The housing

Newer condos, townhomes and some single-family homes. HOA dues are standard here, and many homes carry special assessments on the tax bill, so read the bill before you compare prices with older neighborhoods.

Good fit if

You want new construction, parks and a short walk to work.

Think twice if

You want character, older architecture or no HOA.
