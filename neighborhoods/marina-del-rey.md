What it’s like

Life here runs around the water: paddleboarding in the harbor, calm water at Mother's Beach, the bike path to Venice and Playa del Rey, and sunset walks along the main channel.

Marina del Rey is unincorporated LA County, built around a small-craft harbor that opened in the 1960s. The county owns most of the land, so most buildings sit on long-term ground leases.

## The homes, the rules and who it fits

### The housing

Mostly apartments and condo towers. Many condos are leasehold, so the length of the ground lease affects financing and value. Ask about the lease term before you fall in love with the view.

**Good fit if**

You want water views, the bike path and an easy condo lifestyle.

**Think twice if**

You want to own your land outright or have a yard.
