What it’s like

Life runs along PCH: Point Dume and Zuma in the west, the Malibu Country Mart and Cross Creek in the middle, Carbon Beach and the pier to the east, Pepperdine up on the hill. It's quiet, spread out, and you drive everywhere.

Malibu is its own city stretching along about 21 miles of coastline west of Pacific Palisades. Parts of eastern Malibu burned in the January 2025 Palisades Fire.

## The homes, the rules and who it fits

### The housing

Beachfront, bluff-top and canyon homes, many on large lots. Most of Malibu runs on septic systems instead of sewer, coastal permits add time to projects, and fire insurance is a real line item. Inspect all three before you write an offer.

**Good fit if**

You want privacy, ocean and space, and the drive into town doesn't bother you.

**Think twice if**

You want walkability or a short commute anywhere.
