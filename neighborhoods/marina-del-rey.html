What it’s like

Life here runs around the water: paddleboarding in the harbor, calm water at Mother's Beach, the bike path to Venice and Playa del Rey, and sunset walks along the main channel.

Marina del Rey is unincorporated LA County, built around a small-craft harbor that opened in the 1960s. The county owns most of the land, so most buildings sit on long-term ground leases.

Where to eat, drink and go in Marina del Rey

Marina del Rey wraps around North America's largest man-made small-craft harbor, with waterfront restaurants, a calm swimming beach and a bike path that runs to Venice and Playa del Rey. The best food is often a block off the water, along Washington Boulevard and in the strip malls inland.

Record king tides and big surf hit the coast on Thanksgiving morning

An extreme high tide of about 8 feet arrived Thursday morning, with a coastal flood advisory for the Malibu coast and LA County beaches through noon and a high surf warning until 9 p.m. Forecasters expect waves of roughly 6 to 11 feet with strong rip currents, and the LA Harbor tied its all-time high-water mark. If you're heading to the beach this weekend, check tide times before parking in beach lots and stay off the rocks and jetties.
