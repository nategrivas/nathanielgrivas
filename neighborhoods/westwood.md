What it’s like

Westwood Village has the old movie palaces, the Hammer Museum and campus energy. The Wilshire Corridor is condo living with doormen. North of Wilshire, the residential streets are quiet and tree-lined. The Metro D Line subway extension to Westwood is under construction.

Westwood is the City of LA neighborhood around UCLA, from the Wilshire Corridor high-rises to the single-family streets of Westwood Hills and Holmby Westwood.

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
