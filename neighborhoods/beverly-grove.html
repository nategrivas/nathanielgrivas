Beverly Grove is the City of LA neighborhood between West Hollywood, Beverly Hills and the Fairfax District, home to Cedars-Sinai and the Beverly Center.

What it's like

West Third Street for shops and restaurants, Melrose Place for boutiques, La Cienega's restaurant row, and Cedars-Sinai as a major employer. It's central and very walkable.

The housing

1920s and 1930s Spanish duplexes are the signature, along with single-family homes north of Beverly Boulevard and condos. Duplexes here work for owner-occupants who want rent from the second unit.

Know these

  • West Third StreetShops and restaurants between La Cienega and Fairfax.
  • Melrose PlaceShort, quiet street of boutiques.
  • Cedars-SinaiThe medical center and one of the area's biggest employers.
  • Spanish duplexesTwo units, one lot, and rent that helps pay the mortgage.
Good fit if

You want a central, walkable spot, or a duplex to live in and rent out.

Think twice if

You want a big backyard.
