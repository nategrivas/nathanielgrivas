What it's like

Abbot Kinney Boulevard is the dinner-and-shopping strip. Ocean Front Walk, the skate park and the outdoor courts are the loud, famous part. A few blocks inland the streets go quiet, and the canals are some of the most peaceful blocks in the city.

The full Venice guideHistory, housing, who it fits, taxes and rules+

Venice is part of the City of Los Angeles, between Santa Monica and Marina del Rey. Abbot Kinney built it in 1905 as Venice of America, and LA annexed it in 1926.

The housing

Small lots, walk streets where homes face a pedestrian path, 1920s cottages next to architect-built modern homes. Venice sits in the Coastal Zone, so many projects need a Coastal Development Permit on top of regular permits.

Good fit if

You want to walk to the beach and to dinner, and you like energy around you.

Think twice if

You want quiet weekends or a big yard.
