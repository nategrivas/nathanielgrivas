What it’s like

Sunday means the Mar Vista Farmers Market on Grand View Boulevard. Venice Boulevard has the local restaurants and coffee. Mar Vista Hill gives you ocean views, and the rec center fills up on weekends.

Mar Vista is a City of LA neighborhood between Venice, Culver City and Palms. It grew up after World War II, and it's one of the most active new-construction areas on the Westside.

## Where to eat, drink and go in Mar Vista

Mar Vista is Venice's quieter neighbor, with a walkable stretch of Venice Boulevard around Grand View, a Sunday farmers market and one of the best Japanese markets on the Westside. Its restaurant scene has grown fast, from Taiwanese comfort food on Grand View to a French bistro on Centinela.

## The homes, the rules and who it fits

### The housing

Post-war single-family homes on lots around 5,000 to 7,000 square feet, with plenty of remodels, new builds and ADUs. The Mar Vista Tract, designed by Gregory Ain in 1948, is a protected historic district.

**Good fit if**

You want a house with a yard on the Westside and quick freeway access.

**Think twice if**

You want to walk to most of what you need.

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
