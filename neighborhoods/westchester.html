What it’s like

Suburban and steady: Kentwood and Westport Heights for houses, the Sepulveda and Manchester corridors for errands, Westchester Park for sports. The closer you get to LAX, the more you hear the planes.

Westchester grew up in the 1940s around LAX and the aircraft plants. It's a City of LA neighborhood with Loyola Marymount University on the bluff above Playa Vista.
