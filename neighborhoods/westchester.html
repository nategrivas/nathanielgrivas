The full Westchester guideHistory, housing, who it fits, taxes and rules+

Westchester grew up in the 1940s around LAX and the aircraft plants. It's a City of LA neighborhood with Loyola Marymount University on the bluff above Playa Vista.

The housing

1940s and 1950s single-family homes on lots around 5,000 to 7,000 square feet, with a lot of remodels and new builds. One of the better values for a Westside house with a yard.

Good fit if

You want a house and a yard on the Westside for less, and you fly a lot.

Think twice if

Plane noise bothers you.
