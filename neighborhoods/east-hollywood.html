The story of East Hollywood

UCLA's first campus and Frank Lloyd Wright's Hollyhock House

East Hollywood is where Los Angeles went to college. The state normal school moved to Vermont Avenue in 1914, became the first home of UCLA in 1919 and, when UCLA left for Westwood in 1929, Los Angeles City College. Up the hill, oil heiress Aline Barnsdall commissioned Frank Lloyd Wright's first Los Angeles house, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and along Hollywood Boulevard the city designated Thai Town and Little Armenia.

  1. 1914

    The Los Angeles State Normal School, a teachers college, moves from downtown to Vermont Avenue.

  2. 1919

  3. 1921

  4. 1927

  5. 1929

  7. 2000

  8. 2019

IVermont Avenue

UCLA's first home

76-0UCLA's score against USC in the first game, 1929

In 1914 the state normal school, which trained teachers, moved to a new Romanesque campus on Vermont Avenue. In 1919 it became the Southern Branch of the University of California, the school that grew into UCLA. Within ten years it had outgrown the site and moved to Westwood. The Board of Education bought the Vermont campus for $700,000, and Los Angeles Junior College opened there in September 1929. It became Los Angeles City College in 1938.

IIOlive Hill

Hollyhock House

2019UNESCO World Heritage listing

Oil heiress Aline Barnsdall bought Olive Hill to build an arts colony and hired Frank Lloyd Wright to design her house. Finished in 1921 and decorated with stylized hollyhocks, her favorite flower, it was Wright's first commission in Los Angeles. Barnsdall gave the house and the hilltop to the city in 1927, and in 2019 Hollyhock House joined seven other Wright buildings on the UNESCO World Heritage List.

IIIHollywood Boulevard east

Thai Town and Little Armenia

1999Thai Town is designated

East of the 101, Hollywood Boulevard is lined with restaurants, markets, bakeries and churches. The city formally recognized the stretch as Thai Town in 1999, the first such designation in the country, and the blocks around it as Little Armenia in 2000.
