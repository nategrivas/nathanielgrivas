What it’s like

The reservoir walking path and the Meadow on weekends, Sunset Junction's coffee and shops, public stairways through the hills, and some of LA's best modern architecture by Neutra, Schindler and Lautner.

Silver Lake is a City of LA neighborhood built around the Silver Lake Reservoir, between Los Feliz and Echo Park.

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
