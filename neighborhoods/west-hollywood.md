What it’s like

The Sunset Strip's music venues, the Rainbow District's nightlife on Santa Monica Boulevard, the Design District around Melrose and Robertson, and very walkable residential streets in between.

West Hollywood is its own city of about 1.9 square miles between Beverly Hills and Hollywood. It has its own rent stabilization rules and no city transfer tax beyond the county's 0.11%.
