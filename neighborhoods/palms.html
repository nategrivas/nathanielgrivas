What it’s like

Two Metro E Line stations, a short drive to Culver City's restaurants, and a lot of apartment buildings. It's practical, central and busy.

Palms is one of the oldest neighborhoods on LA's Westside, founded in 1886 and annexed to the City of LA in 1915. It sits between Culver City, Mar Vista, Cheviot Hills and West LA.
