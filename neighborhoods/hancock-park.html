The full Hancock Park & Larchmont guideHistory, housing, who it fits, taxes and rules+

Hancock Park is one of LA's oldest wealthy neighborhoods, developed in the 1920s on land owned by the Hancock family. Larchmont Village is its walkable main street.

The housing

1920s Tudor, Spanish and Colonial estates. Hancock Park is an HPOZ, so exterior changes go through historic review.

Good fit if

You want an old-LA house on a quiet, walkable street.

Think twice if

You want a modern build with no review process.
