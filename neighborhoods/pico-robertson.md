What it’s like

Pico Boulevard is lined with kosher bakeries, restaurants and small shops, and the neighborhood is easy to walk. Beverlywood to the south is quieter and almost all single-family.

Pico-Robertson and Beverlywood sit just south and east of Beverly Hills in the City of LA, centered on Pico and Robertson Boulevards.

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
