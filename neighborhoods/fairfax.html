What it’s like

The Original Farmers Market has been at Third and Fairfax since 1934. The Grove is next door, Pan Pacific Park has fields and a rec center, Canter's Deli is open late, and Fairfax Avenue is a streetwear destination.

The Fairfax District is a City of LA neighborhood around Fairfax Avenue between Melrose and Wilshire, next to The Grove and CBS Television City.

What is Fairfax District like?

The Original Farmers Market has been at Third and Fairfax since 1934. The Grove is next door, Pan Pacific Park has fields and a rec center, Canter's Deli is open late, and Fairfax Avenue is a streetwear destination.

Is Fairfax District its own city?

What ZIP codes cover Fairfax District?

What kind of homes are in Fairfax District?

1920s and 1930s Spanish duplexes and fourplexes, many under rent stabilization, plus single-family homes on the side streets. Duplex-to-single-family conversions are common.
