Tarzana is a City of LA neighborhood in the southwest Valley, named after Edgar Rice Burroughs' ranch. Burroughs wrote the Tarzan books.

What it's like

Ventura Boulevard for errands and restaurants, Providence Tarzana Medical Center, the rec center, and hillside streets climbing toward Mulholland.

The housing

Hillside homes with views south of Ventura, ranch homes north of it, and condos along Ventura and Reseda Boulevards.

Know these

  • South of VenturaHillside streets with views.
  • Tarzana Recreation CenterFields, courts and community classes.
  • Providence TarzanaThe neighborhood's medical center.
  • MulhollandThe ridge road on the south edge.
Good fit if

You want more house and yard for the money than Encino.

Think twice if

You need to be over the hill fast every day.
