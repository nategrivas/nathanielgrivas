What it’s like

Westfield Century City for shopping and dinner, wide sidewalks, and a straight shot to Beverly Hills and Westwood. A Metro D Line station is under construction on Constellation Boulevard.

Century City was built starting in the 1960s on land that was once the 20th Century Fox backlot. The studio is still next door, and the district is a City of LA neighborhood of office and condo towers.
