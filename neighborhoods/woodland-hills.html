What it’s like

Warner Center has Westfield Topanga and The Village for shopping and dinner. Topanga State Park trails start in the hills to the south, and Pierce College sits on the north side. Summers run hot.

Woodland Hills is a City of LA neighborhood at the west end of the Valley, next to Calabasas and Topanga.
