The full West Hollywood guideHistory, housing, who it fits, taxes and rules+

West Hollywood is its own city of about 1.9 square miles between Beverly Hills and Hollywood. It has its own rent stabilization rules and no city transfer tax beyond the county's 0.11%.

The housing

Condos, rent-stabilized apartments, small houses in the Norma Triangle and on the east side, and hillside homes above Sunset.

Good fit if

You want to walk to dinner and stay out late.

Think twice if

You want a yard or a quiet Saturday night.
