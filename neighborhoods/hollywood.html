Busy and loud around Hollywood and Highland, calmer on the side streets. The Hollywood Bowl, Hollywood Forever's summer movies, and studios like Paramount keep it a working entertainment neighborhood.

Hollywood is the City of LA neighborhood around Hollywood Boulevard and Sunset, with Paramount Pictures on Melrose and Metro B Line stations under the boulevard.

Where to eat, drink and go in Hollywood

See them on the map →

Hollywood is LA's entertainment district, with historic movie palaces, the Walk of Fame, the Bowl and the Pantages, plus serious restaurants on Melrose, Highland and Sunset. Runyon Canyon and the hills around the Hollywood Sign start just above the flats.

What is Hollywood like?

Is Hollywood its own city?
