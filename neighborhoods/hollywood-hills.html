What it’s like

Runyon Canyon for the crowded hike, Lake Hollywood for the quiet loop, the Hollywood Bowl for summer nights, and Mulholland for the drive. You're up in the hills and still close to the Sunset Strip.

The Hollywood Hills run along the Santa Monica Mountains above Hollywood and West Hollywood, from the Bird Streets to Laurel Canyon and Lake Hollywood. Almost all of it is in the City of LA.
