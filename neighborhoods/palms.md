What it’s like

Two Metro E Line stations, a short drive to Culver City's restaurants, and a lot of apartment buildings. It's practical, central and busy.

Palms is one of the oldest neighborhoods on LA's Westside, founded in 1886 and annexed to the City of LA in 1915. It sits between Culver City, Mar Vista, Cheviot Hills and West LA.

### The housing

Mostly apartments and condos, with a large share under LA's rent stabilization rules. Pockets of single-family homes sit south of the 10.

**Good fit if**

**Think twice if**

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
