What it’s like

Echo Park Lake with the paddle boats and lotus beds, Dodger Stadium up the hill, Elysian Park's trails, and a strip of bars, music venues and restaurants on Sunset.

Echo Park is a City of LA neighborhood northwest of Downtown, between Silver Lake and Elysian Park.
