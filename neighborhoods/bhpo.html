What it’s like

Coldwater Canyon, Benedict Canyon and Franklin Canyon, with Mulholland on top. Franklin Canyon Park has a lake and trails, and TreePeople's park sits at Coldwater and Mulholland.

Beverly Hills Post Office, or BHPO, is the hillside area above Beverly Hills that uses the 90210 ZIP code but sits in the City of Los Angeles. That means LA's transfer tax, Measure ULA and LAUSD schools apply here.

The homes, the rules and who it fits

The housing

Hillside homes and view lots. LA's Baseline Hillside Ordinance limits how big you can build, and most of the area is in a high fire hazard zone, which affects insurance.

Good fit if

You want privacy and views 10 minutes from Beverly Hills.

Think twice if

You want to walk anywhere, or you assumed 90210 means Beverly Hills schools.
