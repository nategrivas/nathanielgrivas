What it’s like

It feels like a beach town from a few decades ago: a short strip of bars and restaurants on Culver Boulevard, Del Rey Lagoon park, and Dockweiler's fire rings at night. The south end sits under the LAX flight path.

Playa del Rey is a small beach community in the City of LA, between Marina del Rey and LAX, with the Ballona Wetlands on one side and Dockweiler State Beach on the other.

Record king tides and big surf hit the coast on Thanksgiving morning
