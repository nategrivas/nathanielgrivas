What it’s like

Cheviot Hills Recreation Center has tennis courts, a pool and even an archery range. Rancho Park Golf Course is one of the busiest public courses in the city. Pico Boulevard has the local restaurants, and the E Line stops at Westwood/Rancho Park.

Cheviot Hills and Rancho Park are City of LA neighborhoods between Century City, Palms and Culver City, wrapped around the Rancho Park golf course and Hillcrest Country Club.
