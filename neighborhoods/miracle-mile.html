What it’s like

Museums at your door, Park La Brea's towers, and Metro D Line subway stations on Wilshire. The residential streets south of Wilshire are calm and walkable.

Miracle Mile is the City of LA neighborhood along Wilshire Boulevard between Fairfax and La Brea, home to LACMA, the Academy Museum, the Petersen and the La Brea Tar Pits.
