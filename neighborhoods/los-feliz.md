## The pockets of Los Feliz

[Every LA pocket on one map →](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/map)

In Los Angeles a single street can change the price of a house. Los Feliz is really 6 smaller neighborhoods, and these are the lines between them, drawn street by street. Dashed outlines are the pockets next door.

### Los Feliz Flats

Los Feliz Boulevard to Hollywood Boulevard, Western to Vermont, around Laughlin Park

The Los Feliz flats: Spanish and Tudor homes and classic apartment buildings on a tree-lined grid.

### Laughlin Park

Franklin Avenue to Los Feliz Boulevard, Hobart to Normandie

Laughlin Park: a small gated hill of historic estates above Franklin Avenue.

### Los Feliz Village

Los Feliz Boulevard to Hollywood Boulevard, Vermont to Hillhurst

Los Feliz Village: the shops and cafes of Vermont and Hillhurst, with homes and apartments between them.

### Los Feliz Estates

North of Los Feliz Boulevard, west of Vermont toward Fern Dell

Los Feliz Estates: hillside homes north of the boulevard near the Griffith Park entrance at Fern Dell.

### Los Feliz Oaks

North of Los Feliz Boulevard around Glendower, Bryn Mawr and Commonwealth

Los Feliz Oaks: large hillside homes, including the Ennis House, on the slopes below Griffith Observatory.

### Franklin Hills

Hillhurst east to Hyperion, Los Feliz Boulevard south to Sunset

Franklin Hills: hillside homes and public stairways around the Shakespeare Bridge.

### Culture & shows

### Griffith Observatory

Free

2800 E Observatory Rd

LA's Art Deco hilltop observatory, with free astronomy exhibits, a planetarium and the city's best sunset view.

**Do:** Walk up the two-mile trail instead of driving, then stay for sunset and a planetarium show.

[Directions ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Observatory+2800+E+Observatory+Rd+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)

### Greek Theatre

$$$

2700 N Vermont Ave

A 1929 open-air amphitheater in a canyon, and one of the best places in LA to see a summer show.

**Do:** Walk or rideshare. The stacked parking can take longer than the concert.

The story of Los Feliz

## Griffith's gift and the hillside of landmark houses

Los Feliz takes its name from Rancho Los Feliz. In 1896 Griffith J. Griffith gave Los Angeles more than 3,000 acres of it for a park, and the hillsides below filled with some of the most important houses in America, including a Frank Lloyd Wright temple of textile blocks and a Richard Neutra house that announced modern architecture to the country. Walt Disney started his first Los Angeles studio a few blocks away.

### Griffith's gift

**3,015** acres donated for Griffith Park

Griffith J. Griffith, a mining speculator, bought part of the old Rancho Los Feliz and in 1896 gave 3,015 acres of it to Los Angeles for a public park. His will later paid for the Greek Theatre and the Griffith Observatory, which opened in 1935 with the promise that anyone could look through its telescopes for free. Admission to the observatory is still free.

IIThe hillside

### Houses that changed architecture

**1924** Ennis House

The slopes below the park became a showcase. Frank Lloyd Wright's Ennis House of 1924, built of patterned concrete blocks, looks like a Mayan temple and has appeared in films from Blade Runner on. Five years later Richard Neutra finished the Lovell Health House, a steel frame hung with glass and concrete on a steep lot, one of the first fully modern houses in the United States. Both are on the hills above Los Feliz Boulevard.

III1923

### Where Disney began

**1923** Disney's first LA studio

In 1923 Walt Disney and his brother Roy set up shop on Kingswell Avenue, first in an uncle's garage and then in a small storefront nearby. They moved to a bigger studio on Hyperion Avenue in Silver Lake in 1926, but Los Feliz is where the company that would make Mickey Mouse began.
