What it’s like

Los Feliz Village on Vermont and Hillhurst has the restaurants, bars and bookstores. The park has hiking, golf, the zoo and the observatory. Frank Lloyd Wright's Ennis House sits on the hill.

Los Feliz is a City of LA neighborhood at the foot of Griffith Park, one of the largest city parks in the country, with the Griffith Observatory and the Greek Theatre in the hills above.

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
