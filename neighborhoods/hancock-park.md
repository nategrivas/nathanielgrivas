What it’s like

Wide lawns, big trees and no sidewalk clutter. Larchmont Boulevard has the coffee, the farmers market and the small shops. The Wilshire Country Club sits in the middle.

Hancock Park is one of LA's oldest wealthy neighborhoods, developed in the 1920s on land owned by the Hancock family. Larchmont Village is its walkable main street.

## The homes, the rules and who it fits

### The housing

1920s Tudor, Spanish and Colonial estates. Hancock Park is an HPOZ, so exterior changes go through historic review.

**Good fit if**

The rules here

- **Jurisdiction:** City of Los Angeles
- **Transfer tax:** 0.45% city + 0.11% county
- **Measure ULA:** Applies Yes. 4% of the full price over $5.4M, 5.5% at $10.9M and up (2026 thresholds)
- **Rent control:** LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance covers most apartments in buildings built before October 1, 1978
- **Public schools:** Los Angeles Unified (LAUSD)
