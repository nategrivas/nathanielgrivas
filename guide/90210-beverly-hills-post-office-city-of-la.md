# The 90210 homes that pay LA's mansion tax

By [Nathaniel Grivas](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/about), REALTOR®
November 25, 2026

The hills above Beverly Hills, from Coldwater Canyon to Benedict Canyon and Franklin Canyon, use the 90210 ZIP code. They're in the City of Los Angeles. Locals call the area Beverly Hills Post Office, or BHPO, after the post office that delivers its mail.

The ZIP decides where your mail goes. The city line decides your taxes, your schools and your building rules.

## What changes at the city line

- **Transfer tax:** Beverly Hills charges no city transfer tax, only the county's 0.11%. BHPO pays the City of LA's 0.45% plus Measure ULA on sales above $5.4M.
- **Schools:** Beverly Hills homes feed into Beverly Hills Unified. BHPO homes are in LAUSD.
- **City services:** Beverly Hills has its own police and fire departments. BHPO is covered by LAPD and LAFD.
- **Building rules:** BHPO falls under LA's Baseline Hillside Ordinance, which limits how big you can build on a hillside lot.

## The $267,000 example

Two homes sell for $6,000,000 each, one in the Beverly Hills flats and one up the canyon in BHPO:

- **Beverly Hills:** county transfer tax of about $6,600. That's it.
- **BHPO:** about $6,600 county, $27,000 City of LA, and $240,000 in ULA. Total: about $273,600.

Same ZIP, same price, $267,000 apart. Buyers pricing a BHPO home against Beverly Hills comps should know which side of the line each comp sits on, and sellers near $5.4M should price with ULA in mind.

## Other ZIPs that cross city lines

Other ZIPs cross city lines too. 90069 and 90046 cover parts of both West Hollywood and the City of LA, and 90048 splits between West Hollywood and Beverly Grove. A mailing address that says "Los Angeles" or "Beverly Hills" tells you the post office, so check the parcel itself. LA's [ZIMAS](https://zimas.lacity.org/) shows whether an address is in the City of LA.
