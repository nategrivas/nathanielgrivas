# Los Feliz: where to eat, drink and go

Los Feliz sits at the foot of Griffith Park, with two walkable village streets, Hillhurst and Vermont, lined with cafes, old-school Italian spots, bookstores and a century-old movie palace. It's also the easiest way up to the Observatory and the Greek Theatre.

### Coffee

### Maru Coffee

$$

1936 Hillhurst Ave

A minimalist espresso bar that many coffee people rank among the best in LA. The line moves faster than it looks.

**Order:** The cream top or a vanilla latte. It's small, so plan to take it to go.

### Handles Coffee

$

4459 Sunset Blvd

A two-story cafe at the bottom of Los Feliz with strong, espresso-heavy drinks and a friendly counter.

### Breakfast

### Alcove Cafe and Bakery

$$

1929 Hillhurst Ave

A pair of historic bungalows with a big shaded patio, and Los Feliz's go-to brunch for decades. Breakfast runs until 5pm.

**Order:** Something from the pastry case and a breakfast plate on the patio. Valet helps with parking.

### Live music

### Greek Theatre

$$$

2700 N Vermont Ave

A 1929 open-air amphitheater in a canyon, and one of the best places in LA to see a summer show.

**Do:** Walk or rideshare. The stacked parking can take longer than the concert.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Greek+Theatre+2700+N+Vermont+Ave+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-griffith-park) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz)

### Movies

### Vista Theatre

$$

4473 Sunset Dr

A century-old single-screen movie palace with an Egyptian interior. Every other row was removed, so there's real legroom.

**Do:** A matinee with real butter on the popcorn.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vista+Theatre+4473+Sunset+Dr+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)

### Landmarks & sights

### Griffith Observatory

Free

2800 E Observatory Rd

LA's Art Deco hilltop observatory, with free astronomy exhibits, a planetarium and the city's best sunset view.

**Do:** Walk up the two-mile trail instead of driving, then stay for sunset and a planetarium show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Observatory+2800+E+Observatory+Rd+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)
