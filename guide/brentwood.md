# Brentwood: where to eat, drink and go

Brentwood's center is San Vicente Boulevard, a coral-tree-lined median with a run of cafes and Italian restaurants, plus Barrington Court just off it. The Getty sits on the hill above, the Country Mart anchors the west end, and the trails up Mandeville Canyon start a few minutes away.

### Museums

### The Getty

Free

1200 Getty Center Dr

The hilltop museum with world-class art, gardens and views from downtown to the ocean. Admission is free; parking is paid.

**Do:** Reserve a time online, ride the tram up and finish in the Central Garden. Go before March 15, 2027, when it closes for about a year of renovation.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Getty+1200+Getty+Center+Dr+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) [Getty](https://www.getty.edu/about/building-gettys-future/)

### Hikes & nature

### Westridge Trailhead and Mandeville Canyon

Free

2660 Westridge Rd

A dog-friendly ridge hike with an easy fire road and a steeper parallel trail, and views from downtown to the ocean.

**Do:** Go early or at sunset. There's no shade and little parking.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westridge+Trailhead+and+Mandeville+Canyon+2660+Westridge+Rd+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)

Dog-friendly Fitness & outdoor sports

### San Vicente Mountain Park

Free

17500 Mulholland Dr

A ridgetop park at an old Cold War Nike missile site with a lookout tower and huge views.

**Do:** Hike in from the Westridge trail, about four miles each way.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=San+Vicente+Mountain+Park+17500+Mulholland+Dr+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)
