# The best markets and farmers markets in Los Angeles

14 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Casita Basqueria

$$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 3730 Cross Creek Rd

A tiny Spanish market making sandwiches on imported bread.

**Order:** Go early; the daily sandwiches sell out.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Casita+Basqueria+3730+Cross+Creek+Rd+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-malibu-restaurants)

Lunch

### Erewhon Palisades

$$$

[Pacific Palisades](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/pacific-palisades/#eat) · Palisades Village

The luxury grocer, back in the Village with its smoothie and hot bars.

**Do:** A smoothie and lunch from the hot bar.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Erewhon+Palisades+Palisades+Village+Pacific+Palisades+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Los Angeles Magazine](https://lamag.com/news/palisades-village-re-opens-celebrating-a-sense-of-normalcy-and-resilience/)

Healthy & plant-based

### Santa Monica Farmers Market

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · Arizona Ave at 2nd St

The Wednesday downtown market where LA chefs shop. Guidebooks single it out as one of the city's best.

**Do:** Go Wednesday morning for the full market.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Farmers+Market+Arizona+Ave+at+2nd+St+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica) [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/north-america/usa/california/los-angeles/neighborhoods/santa-monica-and-the-beaches/santa-monica/restaurants)

### Abbot Kinney Blvd

$$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Abbot Kinney Blvd

Venice's most stylish street: boutiques, galleries, restaurants and bars along a walkable mile.

**Do:** Artists & Fleas runs Saturdays 11am to 5pm at Abbot Kinney and Westminster.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Abbot+Kinney+Blvd+Abbot+Kinney+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

### Westside

### Farmshop

$$$

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/brentwood/#eat) · 225 26th St

The all-day hangout of the Brentwood Country Mart, with a market for picnic supplies.

**Order:** The poached chicken salad.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Farmshop+225+26th+St+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-restaurants-brentwood)

Brunch Lunch

### Westfield Century City

$$

[Century City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/century-city/#eat) · 10250 Santa Monica Blvd

A big open-air mall with a movie theater, a food hall and Eataly's multi-level Italian market.

**Do:** Go on a weekday; weekend parking gets slow.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westfield+Century+City+10250+Santa+Monica+Blvd+Century+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Century+City+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Citizen Public Market

$$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/culver-city/#eat) · 9355 Culver Blvd

A downtown food hall with a laid-back rooftop bar.

**Order:** Fried chicken, then drinks on the roof.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Citizen+Public+Market+9355+Culver+Blvd+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/restaurants/best-culver-city-restaurants)

Lunch Rooftops & views

### Mitsuwa Marketplace

$

[Mar Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/mar-vista/#eat) · 3760 S Centinela Ave

A Japanese supermarket with a busy food court and a bookstore full of manga and stationery.

**Do:** Ramen or tempura in the food court, then snacks and mochi from the market.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mitsuwa+Marketplace+3760+S+Centinela+Ave+Mar+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://timeout.com/los-angeles/mar-vista) [Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mar+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Mar Vista Farmers Market

Free

[Mar Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/mar-vista/#eat) · Grand View Blvd and Venice Blvd

A Sunday morning market with produce, flowers, prepared food, music and kids' activities.

**Do:** Go early for produce and stay for empanadas.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mar+Vista+Farmers+Market+Grand+View+Blvd+and+Venice+Blvd+Mar+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://timeout.com/los-angeles/mar-vista)

Kids & family

### Nijiya Market and Daiso

$

[West LA & Sawtelle](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-la/#eat) · 2130 Sawtelle Blvd

A Japanese grocery with good bento and sushi, next to the Daiso housewares store.

**Do:** A bento and a can of royal milk tea.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nijiya+Market+and+Daiso+2130+Sawtelle+Blvd+West+LA+%26+Sawtelle+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/restaurants/best-sawtelle-japantown-restaurants) [Discover Los Angeles](https://discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/a-walking-tour-of-sawtelle-japantown)

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### The Cheese Store of Beverly Hills

$$

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills/#eat) · 9705 S Santa Monica Blvd

A fromagerie open for more than 50 years that makes some of the best deli sandwiches in LA.

**Order:** La Zucca or The Dom.

### Central

### Joan's on Third

$$

[Beverly Grove](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-grove/#eat) · 8350 W 3rd St

One of LA's original market cafes, with excellent pastries and a deli counter.

**Order:** A pastry and coffee, or a sandwich from the case.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Joan%27s+on+Third+8350+W+3rd+St+Beverly+Grove+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/usa/los-angeles/west-hollywood/restaurants)

### Original Farmers Market

$

[Fairfax District](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/fairfax/#eat) · 6333 W 3rd St

LA's historic open-air market of food stalls and grocers, open since 1934.

**Do:** Graze: laksa, Brazilian barbecue, oysters and a pie.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Original+Farmers+Market+6333+W+3rd+St+Fairfax+District+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-restaurants-in-beverly-grove) [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/neighborhoods/beverly-grove)

### Valley

### Wat Thai Food Court

$

[North Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/north-hollywood/#eat) · 8225 Coldwater Canyon Ave

Weekend-only Thai street food stalls on the grounds of a Buddhist temple. You trade cash for tokens to pay.

**Do:** Arrive before noon, when the popular stalls start to sell out.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Wat+Thai+Food+Court+8225+Coldwater+Canyon+Ave+North+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/restaurants-north-hollywood) [Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=North+Hollywood+CA)
