The best markets and farmers markets in Los Angeles

14 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Casita Basqueria

$$

Malibu · 3730 Cross Creek Rd

A tiny Spanish market making sandwiches on imported bread.

Order: Go early; the daily sandwiches sell out.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
Lunch

Erewhon Palisades

$$$

Pacific Palisades · Palisades Village

The luxury grocer, back in the Village with its smoothie and hot bars.

Do: A smoothie and lunch from the hot bar.

Map ↗Sources: Los Angeles Magazine
Healthy & plant-based

Santa Monica Farmers Market

Free

Santa Monica · Arizona Ave at 2nd St

The Wednesday downtown market where LA chefs shop. Guidebooks single it out as one of the city's best.

Do: Go Wednesday morning for the full market.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutFodor's

Abbot Kinney Blvd

$$$

Venice · Abbot Kinney Blvd

Venice's most stylish street: boutiques, galleries, restaurants and bars along a walkable mile.

Do: Artists & Fleas runs Saturdays 11am to 5pm at Abbot Kinney and Westminster.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutDiscover Los Angeles
Shopping

Westside

Farmshop

$$$

Brentwood · 225 26th St

The all-day hangout of the Brentwood Country Mart, with a market for picnic supplies.

Order: The poached chicken salad.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
BrunchLunch

Westfield Century City

$$

Century City · 10250 Santa Monica Blvd

A big open-air mall with a movie theater, a food hall and Eataly's multi-level Italian market.

Do: Go on a weekday; weekend parking gets slow.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)
ShoppingMovies

Citizen Public Market

$$

Culver City · 9355 Culver Blvd

A downtown food hall with a laid-back rooftop bar.

Order: Fried chicken, then drinks on the roof.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
LunchRooftops & views

Mitsuwa Marketplace

$

Mar Vista · 3760 S Centinela Ave

A Japanese supermarket with a busy food court and a bookstore full of manga and stationery.

Do: Ramen or tempura in the food court, then snacks and mochi from the market.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutGoogle Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)
LunchCheap eats

Mar Vista Farmers Market

Free

Mar Vista · Grand View Blvd and Venice Blvd

A Sunday morning market with produce, flowers, prepared food, music and kids' activities.

Do: Go early for produce and stay for empanadas.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Kids & family

Nijiya Market and Daiso

$

West LA & Sawtelle · 2130 Sawtelle Blvd

A Japanese grocery with good bento and sushi, next to the Daiso housewares store.

Do: A bento and a can of royal milk tea.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutDiscover Los Angeles

Beverly Hills & the Hills

The Cheese Store of Beverly Hills

$$

Beverly Hills · 9705 S Santa Monica Blvd

A fromagerie open for more than 50 years that makes some of the best deli sandwiches in LA.

Order: La Zucca or The Dom.

Map ↗Sources: The InfatuationTime Out
Lunch

Central

Joan's on Third

$$

Beverly Grove · 8350 W 3rd St

One of LA's original market cafes, with excellent pastries and a deli counter.

Order: A pastry and coffee, or a sandwich from the case.

Map ↗Sources: Lonely Planet
BreakfastLunchBakeries & dessert

Original Farmers Market

$

Fairfax District · 6333 W 3rd St

LA's historic open-air market of food stalls and grocers, open since 1934.

Do: Graze: laksa, Brazilian barbecue, oysters and a pie.

Map ↗Sources: The InfatuationThe Infatuation
Landmarks & sightsLunchKids & family

Valley

Wat Thai Food Court

$

North Hollywood · 8225 Coldwater Canyon Ave

Weekend-only Thai street food stalls on the grounds of a Buddhist temple. You trade cash for tokens to pay.

Do: Arrive before noon, when the popular stalls start to sell out.

Map ↗Sources: The InfatuationGoogle Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)
