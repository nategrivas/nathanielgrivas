# The best dog-friendly spots in Los Angeles

15 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

[Pacific Palisades](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/pacific-palisades) · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

**Do:** Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Will+Rogers+State+Historic+Park+1501+Will+Rogers+State+Park+Rd+Pacific+Palisades+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pacific+Palisades+CA)

### Santa Monica Brew Works

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 1920 Colorado Ave

One of the few real breweries on the Westside, with a wide beer list. Dogs are welcome on the patio and kids until 8pm.

**Order:** A guava-piña sour, with pizza from Emmy Squared ordered at the table.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Brew+Works+1920+Colorado+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica) [Santa Monica Travel & Tourism](https://www.santamonica.com/nightlife/guide-to-santa-monica-bars/)

### Westside

### Westridge Trailhead and Mandeville Canyon

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/brentwood) · 2660 Westridge Rd

A dog-friendly ridge hike with an easy fire road and a steeper parallel trail, and views from downtown to the ocean.

**Do:** Go early or at sunset. There's no shade and little parking.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westridge+Trailhead+and+Mandeville+Canyon+2660+Westridge+Rd+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Cheviot Hills Recreation Center

Free

[Cheviot Hills & Rancho Park](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/cheviot-hills) · 2551 Motor Ave

A big park with tennis courts, a pool, ball fields, basketball and an off-leash dog area.

**Do:** Book a tennis court early on weekends.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cheviot+Hills+Recreation+Center+2551+Motor+Ave+Cheviot+Hills+%26+Rancho+Park+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cheviot+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Kind Grounds

$

[Mar Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/mar-vista) · 3759 Sawtelle Blvd

A bright little cafe with a roomy patio, creative matcha drinks and a dog-friendly policy.

**Order:** A seasonal matcha or the golden dirty chai.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kind+Grounds+3759+Sawtelle+Blvd+Mar+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mar+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### Franklin Canyon Park

Free

[Beverly Hills Post Office](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/bhpo) · 2600 Franklin Canyon Dr

A quiet canyon park with a lake and duck pond, shady trails and old-growth trees, minutes from Beverly Hills.

**Do:** Walk the lake loop and look for turtles in the pond. Drive slowly; there are stop-sign cameras.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Franklin+Canyon+Park+2600+Franklin+Canyon+Dr+Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+CA)

### Laurel Canyon Dog Park

Free

[Hollywood Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood-hills) · 8260 Mulholland Dr

A big off-leash dog park in a wooded hollow, with a separate area for small dogs.

**Do:** Take the entrance curve slowly; it's steep.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Laurel+Canyon+Dog+Park+8260+Mulholland+Dr+Hollywood+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Hills+CA)

### Central

### Pan Pacific Park

Free

[Fairfax District](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/fairfax) · 7600 Beverly Blvd

A big, shady park next to The Grove with lawns, walking paths and sports facilities.

**Do:** A sunset walk, then dinner at the Farmers Market.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pan+Pacific+Park+7600+Beverly+Blvd+Fairfax+District+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fairfax+District+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Runyon Canyon

Free

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 2000 N Fuller Ave

LA's most famous city hike, with dirt trails and big views right above Hollywood.

**Do:** Go early; use the Mulholland entrance for an easier walk.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Runyon+Canyon+2000+N+Fuller+Ave+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### Lake Hollywood Park

Free

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 3160 Canyon Lake Dr

A grassy park with one of the closest, clearest views of the Hollywood Sign.

**Do:** Photos of the sign, then a picnic.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Hollywood+Park+3160+Canyon+Lake+Dr+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### West Hollywood Park

Free

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/west-hollywood) · 647 N San Vicente Blvd

The city's main park, with a modern playground, courts, a dog park and the library next door.

**Do:** Pickleball or tennis, then the library's rooftop views.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=West+Hollywood+Park+647+N+San+Vicente+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=West+Hollywood+CA) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)

### Eastside

### Griffith Park

Free

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · Vermont Ave entrance

More than 4,000 acres of trails, canyons and picnic lawns right behind the neighborhood.

**Do:** A morning hike from the Vermont side, then brunch on Hillhurst.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Park+Vermont+Ave+entrance+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-griffith-park)

### Silver Lake Reservoir

Free

[Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/silver-lake) · Silver Lake Blvd

The neighborhood's centerpiece: a loop for running, a meadow for picnics and room for dogs.

**Do:** Walk the loop, then eat on the Silver Lake Blvd strip just south.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Silver+Lake+Reservoir+Silver+Lake+Blvd+Silver+Lake+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/silver-lake-guide)

### Valley

### Fryman Canyon

Free

[Studio City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/studio-city) · 3493 Laurel Canyon Blvd (trailhead)

A shady canyon loop with a steady climb and Valley views, popular with dog walkers.

**Do:** Go early; the lot fills fast on weekends.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fryman+Canyon+3493+Laurel+Canyon+Blvd+%28trailhead%29+Studio+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Studio+City+CA)

### Lake Balboa Park

Free

[Van Nuys](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/van-nuys) · 6300 Balboa Blvd

A big park around a lake with a 1.3-mile loop path, paddle boats and plenty of birds.

**Do:** Walk the loop in the morning; come in spring for the cherry blossoms.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Balboa+Park+6300+Balboa+Blvd+Van+Nuys+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Van+Nuys+CA)
