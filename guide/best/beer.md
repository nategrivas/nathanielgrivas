# The best beer bars and breweries in Los Angeles

12 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Firestone Walker Propagator

$$

[Marina del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/marina-del-rey) · 3205 Washington Blvd

A big craft brewery taproom that also shows the games.

**Order:** A flight and a game on the big screens.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Firestone+Walker+Propagator+3205+Washington+Blvd+Marina+del+Rey+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/neighborhoods/marina-del-rey)

Sports bars

### Father's Office

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 1018 Montana Ave

The original gastropub, with a burger critics put near the top of LA's list and a rotating craft beer wall. It gets cramped at peak hours.

**Order:** The burger and a rotating draft. Saturday afternoon is the easy time.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Father%27s+Office+1018+Montana+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-restaurants-in-santa-monica) [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica)

Dinner

### Ye Olde King's Head

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 116 Santa Monica Blvd

A British pub steps from the beach that fills up for soccer.

**Order:** Lager and fish and chips during a Premier League match.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ye+Olde+King%27s+Head+116+Santa+Monica+Blvd+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica)

Sports bars

### The Daily Pint

$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 2310 Pico Blvd

A dim, quirky pub with pool tables and a standout beer and whiskey list.

**Order:** Happy hour, and a whiskey you've never tried.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Daily+Pint+2310+Pico+Blvd+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica)

Dive bars

### Santa Monica Brew Works

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 1920 Colorado Ave

One of the few real breweries on the Westside, with a wide beer list. Dogs are welcome on the patio and kids until 8pm.

**Order:** A guava-piña sour, with pizza from Emmy Squared ordered at the table.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Brew+Works+1920+Colorado+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica) [Santa Monica Travel & Tourism](https://www.santamonica.com/nightlife/guide-to-santa-monica-bars/)

Dog-friendly Kids & family

### Wurstküche

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 625 Lincoln Blvd

A sausage-and-beer hall with communal tables, imported beer and very good Belgian fries. Easy for big groups.

**Order:** Order at the counter: two sausages and fries with dipping sauces.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Wurstk%C3%BCche+625+Lincoln+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice)

Late night

### Westside

### Father's Office

$$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/culver-city) · 3229 Helms Ave

The famous no-substitutions burger and a big craft beer list. This location is 21 and over.

**Order:** The Office burger with caramelized onions and a draft beer.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Father%27s+Office+3229+Helms+Ave+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/restaurants/best-culver-city-restaurants)

Cocktail bars Dinner

### 33 Taps

$$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/culver-city) · 9739 Culver Blvd

A big, friendly sports bar with a long tap list and a dog-friendly patio.

**Order:** Football Sundays and happy hour.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=33+Taps+9739+Culver+Blvd+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Culver+City+CA)

### Central

### The Surly Goat

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/west-hollywood) · 7929 Santa Monica Blvd

WeHo's best beer bar, with a deep tap list, shuffleboard and a side patio.

**Order:** Trivia night and whatever's new on tap.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Surly+Goat+7929+Santa+Monica+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-west-hollywood) [LAist](https://laist.com/news/food/how-to-hit-nine-bars-in-west-hollywood-in-one-night)

### Eastside

### Bia Ôm

$$

[Echo Park](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/echo-park) · 1250 W Sunset Blvd

A weekends-only German beer garden.

**Order:** A pretzel, sausages and the giant schnitzel.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bia+%C3%94m+1250+W+Sunset+Blvd+Echo+Park+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-restaurants-Echo-Park)

Lunch

### Red Lion Tavern

$$

[Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/silver-lake) · 2366 Glendale Blvd

A German tavern open since 1959 with a big back patio.

**Order:** Schnitzel, sausages and a pitcher of German beer.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Red+Lion+Tavern+2366+Glendale+Blvd+Silver+Lake+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/bars/the-best-silver-lake-bars) [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/north-america/usa/california/los-angeles/neighborhoods/los-feliz-and-the-eastside/things-to-do/nightlife)

### Game Changer Brewhouse

$$

[Sherman Oaks](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/sherman-oaks) · 13730 Ventura Blvd

A brewpub built for watching games, with big screens everywhere.

**Order:** Wings and a house beer. Book ahead for big games.
