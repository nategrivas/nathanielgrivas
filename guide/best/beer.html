The best beer bars and breweries in Los Angeles

12 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Firestone Walker Propagator

$$

Marina del Rey · 3205 Washington Blvd

A big craft brewery taproom that also shows the games.

Order: A flight and a game on the big screens.

Father's Office

$$

Santa Monica · 1018 Montana Ave

The original gastropub, with a burger critics put near the top of LA's list and a rotating craft beer wall. It gets cramped at peak hours.

Order: The burger and a rotating draft. Saturday afternoon is the easy time.

Map ↗Sources: The InfatuationThe Infatuation
Dinner

Ye Olde King's Head

$$

Santa Monica · 116 Santa Monica Blvd

A British pub steps from the beach that fills up for soccer.

Order: Lager and fish and chips during a Premier League match.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
Sports bars

The Daily Pint

$

Santa Monica · 2310 Pico Blvd

A dim, quirky pub with pool tables and a standout beer and whiskey list.

Order: Happy hour, and a whiskey you've never tried.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
Dive bars

Santa Monica Brew Works

$$

Santa Monica · 1920 Colorado Ave

One of the few real breweries on the Westside, with a wide beer list. Dogs are welcome on the patio and kids until 8pm.

Order: A guava-piña sour, with pizza from Emmy Squared ordered at the table.

Map ↗Sources: The InfatuationSanta Monica Travel & Tourism
Dog-friendlyKids & family

Wurstküche

$$

Venice · 625 Lincoln Blvd

A sausage-and-beer hall with communal tables, imported beer and very good Belgian fries. Easy for big groups.

Order: Order at the counter: two sausages and fries with dipping sauces.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
