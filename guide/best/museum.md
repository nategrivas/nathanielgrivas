# The best museums in Los Angeles

13 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Getty Villa

Free

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 17985 Pacific Coast Hwy

The Getty's re-created Roman villa, with Greek and Roman antiquities and gardens above the ocean. Free with a timed reservation.

**Do:** Reserve online and pay for parking; pair it with lunch on PCH. It stays open while the Getty Center closes for renovation in 2027, with some Center paintings on view here.

### Camera Obscura

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · 1450 Ocean Ave

A historic optical room that projects the street and ocean onto a table. Offbeat and very Santa Monica.

**Do:** Pop in during a walk through Palisades Park.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Camera+Obscura+1450+Ocean+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica)

### Westside

### The Getty

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/brentwood/#eat) · 1200 Getty Center Dr

The hilltop museum with world-class art, gardens and views from downtown to the ocean. Admission is free; parking is paid.

**Do:** Reserve a time online, ride the tram up and finish in the Central Garden. Go before March 15, 2027, when it closes for about a year of renovation.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Getty+1200+Getty+Center+Dr+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) [Getty](https://www.getty.edu/about/building-gettys-future/)

[Bel Air](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/bel-air/#eat) · 2701 N Sepulveda Blvd

### Eastside

### Griffith Observatory

Free

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/los-feliz/#eat) · 2800 E Observatory Rd

LA's Art Deco hilltop observatory, with free astronomy exhibits, a planetarium and the city's best sunset view.

**Do:** Walk up the two-mile trail instead of driving, then stay for sunset and a planetarium show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Observatory+2800+E+Observatory+Rd+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)
