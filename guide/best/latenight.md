# The best late-night spots in Los Angeles

32 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Not No Bar

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · 2424 Main St

A loud, fun cocktail bar that also happens to make very good bubbly-crusted pizza.

**Order:** A cocktail and a pizza for the table.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Not+No+Bar+2424+Main+St+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-restaurants-in-santa-monica)

### The Bungalow

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · 101 Wilshire Blvd

A Baja beach house of a bar on the Fairmont Miramar grounds, with fire pits, living rooms and a crowd that fills it on weekends. Santa Monica's go-to night out.

**Order:** A mezcal cocktail by the fire pit on a Friday or Saturday night.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Bungalow+101+Wilshire+Blvd+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/bars/the-bungalow) [Fairmont Miramar](https://www.fairmont.com/en/hotels/santa-monica/fairmont-miramar-hotel-bungalows/dining/the-bungalow.html)

### Townhouse & Del Monte Speakeasy

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · 52 Windward Ave

A bar that's more than 100 years old, with a Prohibition-era speakeasy downstairs that books live acts. It gets very loud.

**Order:** A tequila cocktail upstairs, then the band downstairs.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Townhouse+%26+Del+Monte+Speakeasy+52+Windward+Ave+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice)

### The Little Friend

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Venice

A small dance bar with 80s hits, a disco ball, inventive cocktails and natural wine.

**Order:** The Passion (mezcal, hibiscus, passionfruit), then dance.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Little+Friend+Venice+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/bars/best-venice-bars)
