# The best dive bars in Los Angeles

21 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### The Warehouse Restaurant

$$

[Marina del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/marina-del-rey) · 4499 Admiralty Way

A quirky, rowdy nautical-themed classic on the water.

**Order:** A tropical drink with friends.

### The Harbor Room

$

[Playa del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/playa-del-rey) · 195 Culver Blvd

A cash-only locals' bar often called the smallest bar in LA County. There's an ATM inside.

**Order:** A cheap, strong drink at the bar.

### Chez Jay

$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 1657 Ocean Ave

A quirky nautical shack by the pier that's been the classic Santa Monica bar for decades.

**Order:** A strong drink and the butter steak.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chez+Jay+1657+Ocean+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica) [Santa Monica Travel & Tourism](https://www.santamonica.com/nightlife/guide-to-santa-monica-bars/)

### Hinano Cafe

$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 15 Washington Blvd

An unpretentious beach dive open since the 1960s, with pool tables and a burger people swear by. Cash only.

**Order:** A burger and a cold beer after the beach.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hinano+Cafe+15+Washington+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/bars/best-venice-bars) [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-venice-restaurants)

Lunch

### The Brig

$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 1515 Abbot Kinney Blvd

A nearly 70-year-old bar with a retro room, a patio and pool in back. Saturdays get busy.

**Order:** A shot-and-beer combo.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Brig+1515+Abbot+Kinney+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice)

### Venice Whaler

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 10 W Washington Blvd

A two-story bar steps from the sand with better bar food than it needs to have. Weekends are lively.

**Order:** A pitcher upstairs at sunset.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Whaler+10+W+Washington+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice)
