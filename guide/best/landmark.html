The best landmarks in Los Angeles

62 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Neptune's Net

$$

Malibu · 42505 Pacific Coast Hwy

A retro 1960s-style seafood stop at the far end of Malibu, famous with motorcyclists.

Order: Clam chowder or anything fried.

SeafoodCheap eats

Malibu Pier and Surfrider Beach

Free

Malibu · 23000 Pacific Coast Hwy

Malibu's historic pier next to the famous point break.

Do: Watch the surfers from the pier in the morning.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
BeachesFitness & outdoor sports

El Matador State Beach

$

Malibu · 32350 Pacific Coast Hwy

A dramatic cove of sea stacks, arches and small caves at the bottom of a steep path.

Do: Go for sunset, but leave before the lot closes.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Beaches

Getty Villa

Free

Malibu · 17985 Pacific Coast Hwy

The Getty's re-created Roman villa, with Greek and Roman antiquities and gardens above the ocean. Free with a timed reservation.

Do: Reserve online and pay for parking; pair it with lunch on PCH. It stays open while the Getty Center closes for renovation in 2027, with some Center paintings on view here.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)Getty
MuseumsArt & galleries

Palisades Village

$$

Pacific Palisades · 15225 Palisades Village Ln

The neighborhood's walkable town square of shops, restaurants and lawns, reopened in August 2026 after a full restoration.

Do: Walk the lawns, shop, then lunch on Swarthmore.

Map ↗Sources: ABC7Los Angeles MagazineGoogle Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
ShoppingKids & family

Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

Pacific Palisades · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

Do: Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)

Dockweiler State Beach

$

Playa del Rey · 12000 Vista Del Mar

A wide beach with fire pits, a bike path and jets taking off from LAX right overhead.

Do: Arrive early on weekends to claim a fire pit.

Venice Canals

Free

Venice · Off Dell Ave

Four blocks of canal houses and arched footbridges, and the calm counterpoint to the boardwalk.

Do: Walk the loop. You can launch your own non-motorized boat from the Venice Blvd ramp.

The Getty

Free

Brentwood · 1200 Getty Center Dr

The hilltop museum with world-class art, gardens and views from downtown to the ocean. Admission is free; parking is paid.

Do: Reserve a time online, ride the tram up and finish in the Central Garden. Go before March 15, 2027, when it closes for about a year of renovation.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)Getty

Do: Hike in from the Westridge trail, about four miles each way.
