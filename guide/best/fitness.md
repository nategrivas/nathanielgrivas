# The best fitness and outdoor sports in Los Angeles

21 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Malibu Pier and Surfrider Beach

Free

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/malibu) · 23000 Pacific Coast Hwy

Malibu's historic pier next to the famous point break.

**Do:** Watch the surfers from the pier in the morning.

### Zuma Beach

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/malibu) · Zuma Beach

A huge, clean stretch of sand with lifeguards, showers and easy parking right next to the water.

**Do:** A full beach day with kids.

### Mothers Beach

$

[Marina del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/marina-del-rey) · 4101 Admiralty Way

A calm, wave-free harbor beach with a lifeguard, a playground and easy kayak and paddleboard launching.

**Do:** Bring little kids, or launch a paddleboard.

### Santa Monica Beach and the Strand

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · Ocean Front Walk

Wide, sandy beach with the bike path running south to Venice and beyond.

**Do:** Rent a bike and ride to Venice in about 15 minutes.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Beach+and+the+Strand+Ocean+Front+Walk+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica)

### Venice Skatepark

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 1800 Ocean Front Walk

The famous concrete bowls by the sand, often mid-video shoot. Watch from the fence.

**Do:** Go near sunset for the best light.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Skatepark+1800+Ocean+Front+Walk+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

### Venice Beach Boardwalk and the Strand

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · Ocean Front Walk

LA's best people-watching, wide soft sand and the flat bike path that runs about 22 miles up and down the coast.

**Do:** Rent a bike: Santa Monica is about 15 minutes north.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Beach+Boardwalk+and+the+Strand+Ocean+Front+Walk+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

### Westside

### Westridge Trailhead and Mandeville Canyon

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/brentwood) · 2660 Westridge Rd

A dog-friendly ridge hike with an easy fire road and a steeper parallel trail, and views from downtown to the ocean.

**Do:** Go early or at sunset. There's no shade and little parking.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westridge+Trailhead+and+Mandeville+Canyon+2660+Westridge+Rd+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)

Free

**Do:** Go early; use the Mulholland entrance for an easier walk.
