# The best comedy and theater in Los Angeles

8 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Kirk Douglas Theatre

$$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/culver-city/#eat) · 9820 Washington Blvd

A former movie house with its neon sign intact, now staging adventurous theater.

**Do:** Check the season calendar.

### The Wallis

$$

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills/#eat) · 9390 N Santa Monica Blvd

### Central

### Pantages Theatre

$$$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/hollywood/#eat) · 6233 Hollywood Blvd

An Art Deco palace for touring Broadway musicals.

**Do:** Front rows of the mezzanine are the best seats.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pantages+Theatre+6233+Hollywood+Blvd+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### Magic Castle

$$$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/hollywood/#eat) · 7001 Franklin Ave

A private club for magicians in a Victorian mansion, with nightly shows.

**Do:** Get in with a member, a hotel stay or show tickets.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Magic+Castle+7001+Franklin+Ave+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### Hamburger Mary's

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-hollywood/#eat) · 8288 Santa Monica Blvd

The original home of WeHo drag brunch, plus drag bingo nights. You go for the show more than the food.

**Order:** Sunday drag brunch with bottomless mimosas.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hamburger+Mary%27s+8288+Santa+Monica+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood) [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/where-to-brunch-in-west-hollywood)

Brunch

### The Comedy Store

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-hollywood/#eat) · 8433 Sunset Blvd

LA's most famous comedy club, with stand-up every night and surprise drop-ins from big names.

**Do:** Tuesday's Roast Battle.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Comedy+Store+8433+Sunset+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)

Late night

### Laugh Factory

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-hollywood/#eat) · 8001 Sunset Blvd

A Strip comedy club known for celebrity sets.

**Do:** Arrive early on Tuesday for the open mic.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Laugh+Factory+8001+Sunset+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)
