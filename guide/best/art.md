# The best art and galleries in Los Angeles

15 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Getty Villa

Free

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 17985 Pacific Coast Hwy

The Getty's re-created Roman villa, with Greek and Roman antiquities and gardens above the ocean. Free with a timed reservation.

**Do:** Reserve online and pay for parking; pair it with lunch on PCH. It stays open while the Getty Center closes for renovation in 2027, with some Center paintings on view here.

### Mosaic Tile House

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · 1116 Palms Blvd

Two artists' home covered floor to roof in tile, paint and ceramics. Nothing else in LA looks like it.

**Do:** Open Saturdays noon to 3pm by email reservation, about $20.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mosaic+Tile+House+1116+Palms+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice)

### Venice murals

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Windward Ave and the boardwalk

Street art everywhere, including the Touch of Venice mural on Windward that nods to Touch of Evil.

**Do:** Start at Windward and Pacific and walk toward the beach.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+murals+Windward+Ave+and+the+boardwalk+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

### Westside

### The Getty

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/brentwood/#eat) · 1200 Getty Center Dr

The hilltop museum with world-class art, gardens and views from downtown to the ocean. Admission is free; parking is paid.

**Do:** Reserve a time online, ride the tram up and finish in the Central Garden. Go before March 15, 2027, when it closes for about a year of renovation.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Getty+1200+Getty+Center+Dr+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) [Getty](https://www.getty.edu/about/building-gettys-future/)

### Wende Museum

Free

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/culver-city/#eat) · 10808 Culver Blvd

A Cold War museum with Berlin Wall pieces and a sculpture garden.

**Do:** Free admission; check for weekend events.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Wende+Museum+10808+Culver+Blvd+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-culver-city)

### Giant Robot

Free

[West LA & Sawtelle](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/west-la/#eat) · 2015 Sawtelle Blvd

The pop-culture shop that helped start the modern Sawtelle scene, with a gallery for emerging artists.

**Do:** Browse the toys and graphic novels, then the GR2 gallery.

### Hammer Museum

Free

[Westwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/westwood/#eat) · 10899 Wilshire Blvd

UCLA's free contemporary art museum with a courtyard, a strong shop and free screenings and talks.

**Do:** Check the events calendar, then lunch at Lulu.

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### Gagosian

Free

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills/#eat) · 456 N Camden Dr

Exhibitions from major contemporary artists in a gallery a block off Rodeo.

**Do:** Check what's showing before you go.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gagosian+456+N+Camden+Dr+Beverly+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-beverly-hills)

### UTA Artist Space

Free

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/beverly-hills/#eat) · 403 Foothill Rd

A gallery for emerging artists in a space designed by Ai Weiwei.

**Do:** Pair it with the Wallis next door.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=UTA+Artist+Space+403+Foothill+Rd+Beverly+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-beverly-hills)

### LACMA

$$

[Miracle Mile](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/miracle-mile/#eat) · 5905 Wilshire Blvd

LA's biggest art museum, with the new David Geffen Galleries inside and Chris Burden's Urban Light lampposts out front.

**Do:** Urban Light at dusk, then the galleries.

### Barnsdall Art Park and Hollyhock House

$

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/los-feliz/#eat) · 4800 Hollywood Blvd

A hilltop park around Frank Lloyd Wright's Hollyhock House, with summer wine tastings and outdoor movies.

**Do:** Tour the house, then catch the sunset from the lawn.
