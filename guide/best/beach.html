The best beaches in Los Angeles

12 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Paradise Cove Beach Cafe

$$

Malibu · 28128 Pacific Coast Hwy

The only Malibu restaurant actually on the sand. You come for the setting.

Order: A cocktail with your feet in the sand.

LunchCocktail bars

Malibu Pier and Surfrider Beach

Free

Malibu · 23000 Pacific Coast Hwy

Malibu's historic pier next to the famous point break.

Do: Watch the surfers from the pier in the morning.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
Landmarks & sightsFitness & outdoor sports

Zuma Beach

$

Malibu · Zuma Beach

A huge, clean stretch of sand with lifeguards, showers and easy parking right next to the water.

Do: A full beach day with kids.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Kids & familyFitness & outdoor sports

Point Dume State Beach

$

Malibu · 6800 Westward Beach Rd

A beach and a short bluff hike with sweeping views, wildflowers in spring and whales in season.

Do: Hike the headland at sunset.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Hikes & nature

El Matador State Beach

$

Malibu · 32350 Pacific Coast Hwy

A dramatic cove of sea stacks, arches and small caves at the bottom of a steep path.

Do: Go for sunset, but leave before the lot closes.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Landmarks & sights

Mothers Beach

$

Marina del Rey · 4101 Admiralty Way

A calm, wave-free harbor beach with a lifeguard, a playground and easy kayak and paddleboard launching.

Do: Bring little kids, or launch a paddleboard.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Kids & familyFitness & outdoor sports

Dockweiler State Beach

$

Playa del Rey · 12000 Vista Del Mar

A wide beach with fire pits, a bike path and jets taking off from LAX right overhead.

Do: Arrive early on weekends to claim a fire pit.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Landmarks & sights

Big Dean's Ocean Front Cafe

$$

Santa Monica · 1615 Ocean Front Walk

A beach sports bar right by the pier where locals mix with tourists.

Order: A burger and a pitcher on the patio during a game.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
Sports bars

Santa Monica Pier

Free

Santa Monica · Colorado Ave at Ocean Ave

The waterfront icon, with the Ferris wheel, arcade and the end of Route 66.

Do: Go at sunset, then walk north along the sand.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sightsKids & family

Santa Monica Beach and the Strand

Free

Santa Monica · Ocean Front Walk

Wide, sandy beach with the bike path running south to Venice and beyond.

Do: Rent a bike and ride to Venice in about 15 minutes.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Fitness & outdoor sports

Venice Beach Boardwalk and the Strand

Free

Venice · Ocean Front Walk

LA's best people-watching, wide soft sand and the flat bike path that runs about 22 miles up and down the coast.

Do: Rent a bike: Santa Monica is about 15 minutes north.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutDiscover Los Angeles
Landmarks & sightsFitness & outdoor sports

Venice Fishing Pier

Free

Venice · Washington Blvd at the beach

A long, quiet pier at the south end of the boardwalk, good for a sunset walk away from the crowds.

Do: Walk out at sunset, then a burger at Hinano across the street.
