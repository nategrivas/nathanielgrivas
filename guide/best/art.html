The best art and galleries in Los Angeles

15 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Getty Villa

Free

Malibu · 17985 Pacific Coast Hwy

The Getty's re-created Roman villa, with Greek and Roman antiquities and gardens above the ocean. Free with a timed reservation.

Do: Reserve online and pay for parking; pair it with lunch on PCH. It stays open while the Getty Center closes for renovation in 2027, with some Center paintings on view here.

MuseumsLandmarks & sights

Mosaic Tile House

$$

Venice · 1116 Palms Blvd

Two artists' home covered floor to roof in tile, paint and ceramics. Nothing else in LA looks like it.

Do: Open Saturdays noon to 3pm by email reservation, about $20.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Venice murals

Free

Venice · Windward Ave and the boardwalk

Street art everywhere, including the Touch of Venice mural on Windward that nods to Touch of Evil.

Do: Start at Windward and Pacific and walk toward the beach.

Map ↗Sources: Discover Los Angeles
Landmarks & sights

Westside

The Getty

Free

Brentwood · 1200 Getty Center Dr

The hilltop museum with world-class art, gardens and views from downtown to the ocean. Admission is free; parking is paid.

Do: Reserve a time online, ride the tram up and finish in the Central Garden. Go before March 15, 2027, when it closes for about a year of renovation.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)Getty
MuseumsLandmarks & sights

Wende Museum

Free

Culver City · 10808 Culver Blvd

A Cold War museum with Berlin Wall pieces and a sculpture garden.

Do: Free admission; check for weekend events.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Museums

Giant Robot

Free

West LA & Sawtelle · 2015 Sawtelle Blvd

The pop-culture shop that helped start the modern Sawtelle scene, with a gallery for emerging artists.

Do: Browse the toys and graphic novels, then the GR2 gallery.

Map ↗Sources: Discover Los Angeles
Shopping

Hammer Museum

Free

Westwood · 10899 Wilshire Blvd

UCLA's free contemporary art museum with a courtyard, a strong shop and free screenings and talks.

Do: Check the events calendar, then lunch at Lulu.

Beverly Hills & the Hills

Gagosian

Free

Beverly Hills · 456 N Camden Dr

Exhibitions from major contemporary artists in a gallery a block off Rodeo.

Do: Check what's showing before you go.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

UTA Artist Space

Free

Beverly Hills · 403 Foothill Rd

A gallery for emerging artists in a space designed by Ai Weiwei.

Do: Pair it with the Wallis next door.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Central

Sycamore Avenue

$$

Hollywood · Sycamore Ave south of Romaine St

A small strip of restaurants, cafes and boutiques with galleries nearby.

Do: Coffee, shopping, then the gallery on Orange Drive.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Shopping

LACMA

$$

Miracle Mile · 5905 Wilshire Blvd

LA's biggest art museum, with the new David Geffen Galleries inside and Chris Burden's Urban Light lampposts out front.

Do: Urban Light at dusk, then the galleries.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)
MuseumsLandmarks & sights

Schindler House

$

West Hollywood · 835 N Kings Rd

Rudolph Schindler's 1922 house, a modern architecture landmark that hosts art shows.

Do: Tours run Wednesday through Sunday; reserve ahead.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

ONE Gallery

Free

West Hollywood · 626 N Robertson Blvd

A gallery of LGBTQ+ history exhibits on Robertson.

Do: Pair it with Hauser and Wirth nearby.

Eastside

Barnsdall Art Park and Hollyhock House

$

Los Feliz · 4800 Hollywood Blvd

A hilltop park around Frank Lloyd Wright's Hollyhock House, with summer wine tastings and outdoor movies.

Do: Tour the house, then catch the sunset from the lawn.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Wacko and La Luz de Jesus Gallery

Free

Los Feliz · 4633 Hollywood Blvd

A pop-culture oddities shop that gets stranger the deeper you go, with a lowbrow art gallery in back.

Do: Browse the gallery's current show.
