# The best beaches in Los Angeles

11 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Paradise Cove Beach Cafe

$$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 28128 Pacific Coast Hwy

The only Malibu restaurant actually on the sand. You come for the setting.

**Order:** A cocktail with your feet in the sand.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Paradise+Cove+Beach+Cafe+28128+Pacific+Coast+Hwy+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-malibu-restaurants)

Lunch Cocktail bars & lounges

### Malibu Pier and Surfrider Beach

Free

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 23000 Pacific Coast Hwy

Malibu's historic pier next to the famous point break.

**Do:** Watch the surfers from the pier in the morning.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+Pier+and+Surfrider+Beach+23000+Pacific+Coast+Hwy+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-malibu-restaurants)

### Zuma Beach

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · Zuma Beach

A huge, clean stretch of sand with lifeguards, showers and easy parking right next to the water.

**Do:** A full beach day with kids.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Zuma+Beach+Zuma+Beach+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

Kids & family Fitness & outdoor sports

### Point Dume State Beach

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 6800 Westward Beach Rd

A beach and a short bluff hike with sweeping views, wildflowers in spring and whales in season.

**Do:** Hike the headland at sunset.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Point+Dume+State+Beach+6800+Westward+Beach+Rd+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

Hikes & nature

### El Matador State Beach

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 32350 Pacific Coast Hwy

A dramatic cove of sea stacks, arches and small caves at the bottom of a steep path.

**Do:** Go for sunset, but leave before the lot closes.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=El+Matador+State+Beach+32350+Pacific+Coast+Hwy+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

### Mothers Beach

$

[Marina del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/marina-del-rey/#eat) · 4101 Admiralty Way

A calm, wave-free harbor beach with a lifeguard, a playground and easy kayak and paddleboard launching.

**Do:** Bring little kids, or launch a paddleboard.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mothers+Beach+4101+Admiralty+Way+Marina+del+Rey+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Marina+del+Rey+CA)

Kids & family Fitness & outdoor sports

### Dockweiler State Beach

$

[Playa del Rey](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/playa-del-rey/#eat) · 12000 Vista Del Mar

A wide beach with fire pits, a bike path and jets taking off from LAX right overhead.

**Do:** Arrive early on weekends to claim a fire pit.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dockweiler+State+Beach+12000+Vista+Del+Mar+Playa+del+Rey+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+del+Rey+CA)

### Santa Monica Pier

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · Colorado Ave at Ocean Ave

The waterfront icon, with the Ferris wheel, arcade and the end of Route 66.

**Do:** Go at sunset, then walk north along the sand.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Pier+Colorado+Ave+at+Ocean+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica)

Landmarks & sights Kids & family

### Santa Monica Beach and the Strand

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · Ocean Front Walk

Wide, sandy beach with the bike path running south to Venice and beyond.

**Do:** Rent a bike and ride to Venice in about 15 minutes.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Monica+Beach+and+the+Strand+Ocean+Front+Walk+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica)

Fitness & outdoor sports

### Venice Beach Boardwalk and the Strand

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Ocean Front Walk

LA's best people-watching, wide soft sand and the flat bike path that runs about 22 miles up and down the coast.

**Do:** Rent a bike: Santa Monica is about 15 minutes north.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Beach+Boardwalk+and+the+Strand+Ocean+Front+Walk+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

Landmarks & sights Fitness & outdoor sports

### Venice Fishing Pier

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Washington Blvd at the beach

A long, quiet pier at the south end of the boardwalk, good for a sunset walk away from the crowds.

**Do:** Walk out at sunset, then a burger at Hinano across the street.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Fishing+Pier+Washington+Blvd+at+the+beach+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)
