# The best movie theaters in Los Angeles

14 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Runway Playa Vista

$$

[Playa Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/playa-vista) · 12775 W Millennium

The neighborhood's walkable center, with restaurants, a Whole Foods, a movie theater, fire pits and play areas.

**Do:** Dinner and a movie; the theater validates parking.

### Westside

### Westfield Century City

$$

[Century City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/century-city) · 10250 Santa Monica Blvd

A big open-air mall with a movie theater, a food hall and Eataly's multi-level Italian market.

**Do:** Go on a weekday; weekend parking gets slow.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westfield+Century+City+10250+Santa+Monica+Blvd+Century+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Century+City+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Sony Pictures Studios Tour

$$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/culver-city) · 10202 W Washington Blvd

A walking tour of a working studio lot with Art Deco buildings and Wizard of Oz history.

**Do:** Book ahead.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sony+Pictures+Studios+Tour+10202+W+Washington+Blvd+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-culver-city)

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### The Ford

$$

[Hollywood Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood-hills) · 2580 Cahuenga Blvd E

An intimate 1,200-seat outdoor amphitheater built into the hillside, across the freeway from the Hollywood Bowl.

**Do:** Bring your own food and drinks for a summer show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Ford+2580+Cahuenga+Blvd+E+Hollywood+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Hills+CA)

### Central

### The Grove

$$

[Fairfax District](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/fairfax) · 189 The Grove Dr

An outdoor shopping village with a trolley, a fountain show and a movie theater, next to the Farmers Market.

**Do:** Shop, catch a movie, then eat at the market.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Grove+189+The+Grove+Dr+Fairfax+District+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/neighborhoods/beverly-grove)

### TCL Chinese Theatre

$$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 6925 Hollywood Blvd

The historic movie palace with handprints in the forecourt and a giant IMAX screen.

**Do:** Skip the crowded courtyard and see a movie in the main house.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=TCL+Chinese+Theatre+6925+Hollywood+Blvd+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

$$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 6838 Hollywood Blvd

### Academy Museum of Motion Pictures

$$

[Miracle Mile](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/miracle-mile) · 6067 Wilshire Blvd

Floors of film history and rotating exhibits, plus screenings and a terrace that looks toward the Hollywood Hills.

**Do:** Check the screening calendar before you go.

### Eastside

### Vista Theatre

$$

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · 4473 Sunset Dr

A century-old single-screen movie palace with an Egyptian interior. Every other row was removed, so there's real legroom.

**Do:** A matinee with real butter on the popcorn.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vista+Theatre+4473+Sunset+Dr+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)

### Los Feliz 3

$

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · 1822 N Vermont Ave

The neighborhood's other historic cinema, smaller and cheaper than the Vista.

**Do:** A movie, then Goldburger next door.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Los+Feliz+3+1822+N+Vermont+Ave+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz) [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-restaurants-in-los-feliz)
