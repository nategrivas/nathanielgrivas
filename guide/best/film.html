The best movie theaters in Los Angeles

14 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Runway Playa Vista

$$

Playa Vista · 12775 W Millennium

The neighborhood's walkable center, with restaurants, a Whole Foods, a movie theater, fire pits and play areas.

Do: Dinner and a movie; the theater validates parking.

Westfield Century City

$$

Century City · 10250 Santa Monica Blvd

A big open-air mall with a movie theater, a food hall and Eataly's multi-level Italian market.

Do: Go on a weekday; weekend parking gets slow.

Sony Pictures Studios Tour

$$

A walking tour of a working studio lot with Art Deco buildings and Wizard of Oz history.

Do: Book ahead.

Beverly Hills & the Hills

The Ford

$$

Hollywood Hills · 2580 Cahuenga Blvd E

An intimate 1,200-seat outdoor amphitheater built into the hillside, across the freeway from the Hollywood Bowl.

Do: Bring your own food and drinks for a summer show.

Central

The Grove

$$

Fairfax District · 189 The Grove Dr

An outdoor shopping village with a trolley, a fountain show and a movie theater, next to the Farmers Market.

Do: Shop, catch a movie, then eat at the market.

Map ↗Sources: The Infatuation
ShoppingKids & family

TCL Chinese Theatre

$$

Hollywood · 6925 Hollywood Blvd

The historic movie palace with handprints in the forecourt and a giant IMAX screen.

Do: Skip the crowded courtyard and see a movie in the main house.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Egyptian Theatre

$$

Hollywood · 6712 Hollywood Blvd

A beautifully restored 1922 cinema for classics, cult films and festivals.

Do: Catch a nitrate or 70mm screening.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

El Capitan Theatre

$$

Hollywood · 6838 Hollywood Blvd

Disney's movie palace with a live organ, a soda fountain and costume displays downstairs.

Do: A Disney premiere with the kids.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Hollywood Forever Cemetery

$

Hollywood · 6000 Santa Monica Blvd

A historic cemetery that hosts summer movie screenings, concerts and morning yoga.

Do: Bring a blanket for a summer screening.

Paramount Pictures Studio Tour

$$$

Hollywood · 5515 Melrose Ave

A guided tram tour of the last major studio still in Hollywood.

Do: Book ahead and download the tour app.

Academy Museum of Motion Pictures

$$

Miracle Mile · 6067 Wilshire Blvd

Floors of film history and rotating exhibits, plus screenings and a terrace that looks toward the Hollywood Hills.

Do: Check the screening calendar before you go.

Eastside

Vista Theatre

$$

Los Feliz · 4473 Sunset Dr

A century-old single-screen movie palace with an Egyptian interior. Every other row was removed, so there's real legroom.

Do: A matinee with real butter on the popcorn.

Map ↗Sources: Discover Los AngelesTime Out
Landmarks & sights

Los Feliz 3

$

Los Feliz · 1822 N Vermont Ave

The neighborhood's other historic cinema, smaller and cheaper than the Vista.

Do: A movie, then Goldburger next door.

A restored 1920s theater in the Arts District hosting plays, musicals and concerts.
