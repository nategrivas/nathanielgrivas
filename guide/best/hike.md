# The best hikes in Los Angeles

19 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Point Dume State Beach

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/malibu) · 6800 Westward Beach Rd

A beach and a short bluff hike with sweeping views, wildflowers in spring and whales in season.

**Do:** Hike the headland at sunset.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Point+Dume+State+Beach+6800+Westward+Beach+Rd+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

Beaches

### Malibu Creek State Park

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/malibu) · 1925 Las Virgenes Rd

Canyon trails, a rock pool for swimming and the old M*A*S*H filming site.

**Do:** Hike to the M*A*S*H site, about 2.5 miles each way.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+Creek+State+Park+1925+Las+Virgenes+Rd+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

### Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

[Pacific Palisades](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/pacific-palisades) · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

**Do:** Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Will+Rogers+State+Historic+Park+1501+Will+Rogers+State+Park+Rd+Pacific+Palisades+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pacific+Palisades+CA)

### Ballona Wetlands Ecological Reserve

Free

[Playa Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/playa-vista) · Ballona Wetlands

A protected coastal wetland with birding and a walking and bike path that links Marina del Rey and Playa del Rey.

**Do:** An early walk or bike ride along the path. Parts may be closed for restoration.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ballona+Wetlands+Ecological+Reserve+Ballona+Wetlands+Playa+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Vista+CA)

### Westside

### Westridge Trailhead and Mandeville Canyon

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/brentwood) · 2660 Westridge Rd

A dog-friendly ridge hike with an easy fire road and a steeper parallel trail, and views from downtown to the ocean.

**Do:** Go early or at sunset. There's no shade and little parking.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westridge+Trailhead+and+Mandeville+Canyon+2660+Westridge+Rd+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)

### San Vicente Mountain Park

Free

[Brentwood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/brentwood) · 17500 Mulholland Dr

A ridgetop park at an old Cold War Nike missile site with a lookout tower and huge views.

**Do:** Hike in from the Westridge trail, about four miles each way.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=San+Vicente+Mountain+Park+17500+Mulholland+Dr+Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brentwood+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook

Free

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/culver-city) · 6300 Hetzler Rd

A steep 282-step staircase up to a 360-degree view from the ocean to downtown.

**Do:** Climb the stairs at sunset.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Baldwin+Hills+Scenic+Overlook+6300+Hetzler+Rd+Culver+City+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-culver-city)

### Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area

$

[Culver City](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/culver-city) · 4100 S La Cienega Blvd

A large Baldwin Hills park with miles of trails, a fishing lake and big basin views.

**Do:** A loop hike, then a picnic by the lake.

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### Franklin Canyon Park

Free

[Beverly Hills Post Office](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/bhpo) · 2600 Franklin Canyon Dr

A quiet canyon park with a lake and duck pond, shady trails and old-growth trees, minutes from Beverly Hills.

**Do:** Walk the lake loop and look for turtles in the pond. Drive slowly; there are stop-sign cameras.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Franklin+Canyon+Park+2600+Franklin+Canyon+Dr+Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+CA)

### TreePeople (Coldwater Canyon Park)

Free

[Beverly Hills Post Office](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/bhpo) · 12601 Mulholland Dr

A well-kept forested park with wide trails and city and Valley views, run by a nonprofit.

**Do:** Join a guided moonlight hike.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=TreePeople+%28Coldwater+Canyon+Park%29+12601+Mulholland+Dr+Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Beverly+Hills+Post+Office+CA)

### Bronson Caves

Free

[Hollywood Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood-hills) · 3200 Canyon Dr

A short, easy walk to the cave used as the Batcave in the 1960s TV show, with a clear view of the Hollywood Sign.

**Do:** Go early for parking, then keep going on the Brush Canyon trail.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bronson+Caves+3200+Canyon+Dr+Hollywood+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Hills+CA)

### Runyon Canyon

### Eastside

### Griffith Observatory

Free

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · 2800 E Observatory Rd

LA's Art Deco hilltop observatory, with free astronomy exhibits, a planetarium and the city's best sunset view.

**Do:** Walk up the two-mile trail instead of driving, then stay for sunset and a planetarium show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Observatory+2800+E+Observatory+Rd+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz)

### Griffith Park

Free

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · Vermont Ave entrance

More than 4,000 acres of trails, canyons and picnic lawns right behind the neighborhood.

**Do:** A morning hike from the Vermont side, then brunch on Hillhurst.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Griffith+Park+Vermont+Ave+entrance+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-los-feliz) [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-griffith-park)

### Berendo Stairs

Free

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · N Berendo St

A historic public staircase that gives you a real workout on the way toward Griffith Park.

**Do:** Do a few laps early in the morning.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Berendo+Stairs+N+Berendo+St+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz)

Free

[Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/silver-lake) · Throughout the hills

Dozens of public stairways wind through the hillsides, a way to see the neighborhood off the main streets.

**Do:** Link a few for a workout with views.

Free

**Do:** Go early; the lot fills fast on weekends.
