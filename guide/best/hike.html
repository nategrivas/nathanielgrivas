The best hikes in Los Angeles

19 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Point Dume State Beach

$

Malibu · 6800 Westward Beach Rd

A beach and a short bluff hike with sweeping views, wildflowers in spring and whales in season.

Do: Hike the headland at sunset.

Beaches

Malibu Creek State Park

$

Malibu · 1925 Las Virgenes Rd

Canyon trails, a rock pool for swimming and the old M*A*S*H filming site.

Do: Hike to the M*A*S*H site, about 2.5 miles each way.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
ParksKids & family

Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

Pacific Palisades · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

Do: Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
ParksLandmarks & sightsDog-friendly

Ballona Wetlands Ecological Reserve

Free

Playa Vista · Ballona Wetlands

A protected coastal wetland with birding and a walking and bike path that links Marina del Rey and Playa del Rey.

Do: An early walk or bike ride along the path. Parts may be closed for restoration.

Westside

Westridge Trailhead and Mandeville Canyon

Free

Brentwood · 2660 Westridge Rd

A dog-friendly ridge hike with an easy fire road and a steeper parallel trail, and views from downtown to the ocean.

Do: Go early or at sunset. There's no shade and little parking.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Dog-friendlyFitness & outdoor sports

San Vicente Mountain Park

Free

Brentwood · 17500 Mulholland Dr

A ridgetop park at an old Cold War Nike missile site with a lookout tower and huge views.

Do: Hike in from the Westridge trail, about four miles each way.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
ParksLandmarks & sights

Baldwin Hills Scenic Overlook

Free

Culver City · 6300 Hetzler Rd

A steep 282-step staircase up to a 360-degree view from the ocean to downtown.

Do: Climb the stairs at sunset.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Fitness & outdoor sportsLandmarks & sights

Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area

$

Culver City · 4100 S La Cienega Blvd

A large Baldwin Hills park with miles of trails, a fishing lake and big basin views.

Do: A loop hike, then a picnic by the lake.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Beverly Hills & the Hills

Franklin Canyon Park

Free

Beverly Hills Post Office · 2600 Franklin Canyon Dr

A quiet canyon park with a lake and duck pond, shady trails and old-growth trees, minutes from Beverly Hills.

Do: Walk the lake loop and look for turtles in the pond. Drive slowly; there are stop-sign cameras.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
ParksKids & familyDog-friendly

TreePeople (Coldwater Canyon Park)

Free

Beverly Hills Post Office · 12601 Mulholland Dr

A well-kept forested park with wide trails and city and Valley views, run by a nonprofit.

Do: Join a guided moonlight hike.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)
Parks

Bronson Caves

Free

Hollywood Hills · 3200 Canyon Dr

A short, easy walk to the cave used as the Batcave in the 1960s TV show, with a clear view of the Hollywood Sign.

Do: Go early for parking, then keep going on the Brush Canyon trail.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)

Central

Runyon Canyon

Free

Hollywood · 2000 N Fuller Ave

LA's most famous city hike, with dirt trails and big views right above Hollywood.

Do: Go early; use the Mulholland entrance for an easier walk.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Dog-friendlyFitness & outdoor sports

Hollywood Bowl Overlook

Free

Hollywood · Mulholland Dr

A roadside lookout with wide views of the sign and the city.

Do: Arrive early; it closes in the evening.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Sunset Ranch Hollywood

$$$

Hollywood · 3400 N Beachwood Dr

Guided horseback rides in Griffith Park with close views of the sign.

Do: Book the sunset ride.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out

Eastside

Griffith Observatory

Free

Los Feliz · 2800 E Observatory Rd

LA's Art Deco hilltop observatory, with free astronomy exhibits, a planetarium and the city's best sunset view.

Do: Walk up the two-mile trail instead of driving, then stay for sunset and a planetarium show.

Map ↗Sources: Culture TripDiscover Los AngelesTime Out
Landmarks & sightsMuseums

Griffith Park

Free

Los Feliz · Vermont Ave entrance

More than 4,000 acres of trails, canyons and picnic lawns right behind the neighborhood.

Do: A morning hike from the Vermont side, then brunch on Hillhurst.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutTime Out
ParksKids & familyDog-friendly

Berendo Stairs

Free

Los Feliz · N Berendo St

A historic public staircase that gives you a real workout on the way toward Griffith Park.

Do: Do a few laps early in the morning.

Map ↗Sources: Discover Los Angeles
Fitness & outdoor sports

Silver Lake Stairs

Free

Silver Lake · Throughout the hills

Dozens of public stairways wind through the hillsides, a way to see the neighborhood off the main streets.

Do: Link a few for a workout with views.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Fitness & outdoor sports

Valley

Fryman Canyon

Free

Studio City · 3493 Laurel Canyon Blvd (trailhead)

A shady canyon loop with a steady climb and Valley views, popular with dog walkers.

Do: Go early; the lot fills fast on weekends.
