# The best live music in Los Angeles

24 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### The Georgian Room

$$$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 1415 Ocean Ave

A moody basement piano lounge under the Georgian Hotel with a hidden entrance and live singing. Reservations required.

**Order:** A house martini for a special occasion.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Georgian+Room+1415+Ocean+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica)

Cocktail bars Date night

### The Gaslite

$

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/santa-monica) · 2030 Wilshire Blvd

A loud karaoke dive with a dance floor and free popcorn.

**Order:** Sign up for a song and stay to dance.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Gaslite+2030+Wilshire+Blvd+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-bars-in-santa-monica)

Late night Dive bars

### Townhouse & Del Monte Speakeasy

$$

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/venice) · 52 Windward Ave

A bar that's more than 100 years old, with a Prohibition-era speakeasy downstairs that books live acts. It gets very loud.

**Order:** A tequila cocktail upstairs, then the band downstairs.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Townhouse+%26+Del+Monte+Speakeasy+52+Windward+Ave+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/best-bars-in-venice)

Cocktail bars Late night

### Beverly Hills & the Hills

### Skirball Cultural Center

$$

[Bel Air](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/bel-air) · 2701 N Sepulveda Blvd

A cultural center and museum with rotating exhibits, a famous Noah's Ark play space for kids and summer concerts.

**Do:** Bring kids to Noah's Ark; parking is free.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Skirball+Cultural+Center+2701+N+Sepulveda+Blvd+Bel+Air+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bel+Air+Los+Angeles+CA)

Museums Kids & family

### The Wallis

$$

[Beverly Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/beverly-hills) · 9390 N Santa Monica Blvd

A performing arts center in a converted 1933 post office, booking everything from jazz to comedy.

**Do:** Check the Goldsmith Theater lineup.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Wallis+9390+N+Santa+Monica+Blvd+Beverly+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-beverly-hills)

Comedy & theater

### The Ford

$$

[Hollywood Hills](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood-hills) · 2580 Cahuenga Blvd E

An intimate 1,200-seat outdoor amphitheater built into the hillside, across the freeway from the Hollywood Bowl.

**Do:** Bring your own food and drinks for a summer show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Ford+2580+Cahuenga+Blvd+E+Hollywood+Hills+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Hills+CA)

### Central

### Genghis Cohen

$$

[Fairfax District](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/fairfax) · 448 N Fairfax Ave

A kitschy Chinese-American spot made for groups, with a small music room.

**Order:** Kung pao shrimp and lo mein.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Genghis+Cohen+448+N+Fairfax+Ave+Fairfax+District+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://www.theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-restaurants-in-beverly-grove)

Dinner

### Hollywood Bowl

$$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 2301 N Highland Ave

LA's legendary outdoor amphitheater, where a summer picnic and concert is a rite of passage.

**Do:** Bring a picnic and take the Bowl shuttle; parking is limited.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Bowl+2301+N+Highland+Ave+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### Hollywood Forever Cemetery

$

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 6000 Santa Monica Blvd

A historic cemetery that hosts summer movie screenings, concerts and morning yoga.

**Do:** Bring a blanket for a summer screening.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hollywood+Forever+Cemetery+6000+Santa+Monica+Blvd+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-hollywood)

### Amoeba Music

Free

[Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/hollywood) · 6200 Hollywood Blvd

A huge independent record store with vinyl, CDs and movies, plus free in-store shows.

**Do:** Check the in-store calendar.

### Troubadour

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/west-hollywood) · 9081 Santa Monica Blvd

A small, legendary music room with great sound.

**Do:** Stand on the floor and skip the spots under the balcony.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Troubadour+9081+Santa+Monica+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)

### The Roxy

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/west-hollywood) · 9009 Sunset Blvd

A Sunset Strip original with a strong booking calendar. The Rainbow Bar and Grill is next door.

**Do:** Check the calendar and get dinner at the Rainbow before the show.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Roxy+9009+Sunset+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)

### Whisky a Go Go

$$

[West Hollywood](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/west-hollywood) · 8901 Sunset Blvd

The club where the Doors were the house band. These days it books rock, punk and metal.

**Do:** A loud night of live rock.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Whisky+a+Go+Go+8901+Sunset+Blvd+West+Hollywood+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-west-hollywood)

### Greek Theatre

$$$

[Los Feliz](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/los-feliz) · 2700 N Vermont Ave

A 1929 open-air amphitheater in a canyon, and one of the best places in LA to see a summer show.

**Do:** Walk or rideshare. The stacked parking can take longer than the concert.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Greek+Theatre+2700+N+Vermont+Ave+Los+Feliz+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-griffith-park) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/visit/hidden-gems-in-los-feliz) [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/california/articles/the-top-10-things-to-do-and-see-in-los-feliz)

### Manila Inasal

$$

[Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/silver-lake) · 240 Virgil Ave

Fun, inventive takes on Filipino classics, with big portions and live music on weekends.

**Order:** Taro-coconut dip with focaccia and crab-stuffed tortang talong.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Manila+Inasal+240+Virgil+Ave+Silver+Lake+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [The Infatuation](https://theinfatuation.com/los-angeles/guides/the-best-restaurants-in-silver-lake)

$$

[Silver Lake](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/guide/silver-lake) · 4212 W Sunset Blvd
