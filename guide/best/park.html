The best parks in Los Angeles

31 picks across the neighborhoods Nathaniel covers, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

Coast

Malibu Creek State Park

$

Malibu · 1925 Las Virgenes Rd

Canyon trails, a rock pool for swimming and the old M*A*S*H filming site.

Do: Hike to the M*A*S*H site, about 2.5 miles each way.

Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

Pacific Palisades · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

Do: Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)

Ballona Wetlands Ecological Reserve

Free

Playa Vista · Ballona Wetlands

A protected coastal wetland with birding and a walking and bike path that links Marina del Rey and Playa del Rey.

Do: An early walk or bike ride along the path. Parts may be closed for restoration.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)
Hikes & nature

Palisades Park

Free

Santa Monica · Ocean Ave

The bluff-top park with views down the coast and across to the Santa Monica Mountains.

Do: Walk the north end to skip the pier crowds.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Venice Canals

Free

Venice · Off Dell Ave

Four blocks of canal houses and arched footbridges, and the calm counterpoint to the boardwalk.

Do: Walk the loop. You can launch your own non-motorized boat from the Venice Blvd ramp.

Map ↗Sources: Time OutDiscover Los Angeles

Westside

San Vicente Mountain Park

Free

Brentwood · 17500 Mulholland Dr

A ridgetop park at an old Cold War Nike missile site with a lookout tower and huge views.

Do: Hike in from the Westridge trail, about four miles each way.

Cheviot Hills Recreation Center

Free

Cheviot Hills & Rancho Park · 2551 Motor Ave

A big park with tennis courts, a pool, ball fields, basketball and an off-leash dog area.

Do: Book a tennis court early on weekends.

Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area

$

Culver City · 4100 S La Cienega Blvd

A large Baldwin Hills park with miles of trails, a fishing lake and big basin views.

Do: A loop hike, then a picnic by the lake.

Mar Vista Recreation Center

Free

Mar Vista · 11430 Woodbine St

The neighborhood's big park, with a pool, tennis and basketball courts, a roller hockey rink, ball fields and shaded picnic areas.

Do: Swim in the warm months or play tennis.

Beverly Hills & the Hills

Beverly Gardens Park

Free

Beverly Hills · Santa Monica Blvd near Canon Dr

A long, tree-lined park with the Beverly Hills sign everyone photographs.

Do: The Beverly Hills Art Show runs here in May and October.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Greystone Mansion

Free

Beverly Hills · 905 Loma Vista Dr

A 55-room Tudor estate whose gardens and grounds are free to walk.

Do: The interior opens only for events and tours.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Virginia Robinson Gardens

$$

Beverly Hills · 1008 Elden Way

A lavish 1911 estate with formal gardens and a mansion.

Do: Docent-led tours only, booked in advance.

Map ↗Sources: Time Out
Landmarks & sights

Franklin Canyon Park

Free

Beverly Hills Post Office · 2600 Franklin Canyon Dr

A quiet canyon park with a lake and duck pond, shady trails and old-growth trees, minutes from Beverly Hills.

Do: Walk the lake loop and look for turtles in the pond. Drive slowly; there are stop-sign cameras.

Map ↗Sources: Google Maps reviews (local favorites Nathaniel checked)

TreePeople (Coldwater Canyon Park)

Free

Beverly Hills Post Office · 12601 Mulholland Dr

A well-kept forested park with wide trails and city and Valley views, run by a nonprofit.

Do: Join a guided moonlight hike.

Laurel Canyon Dog Park

Free

Hollywood Hills · 8260 Mulholland Dr

A big off-leash dog park in a wooded hollow, with a separate area for small dogs.

Do: Take the entrance curve slowly; it's steep.

Do: Photos of the sign, then a picnic.

La Brea Tar Pits

Free

Miracle Mile · 5801 Wilshire Blvd

Ice Age asphalt seeps and active excavation sites in the park. The museum building is closed for renovation until about 2028, but much of the park stays open.

Do: Walk to the Lake Pit and look for the tar bubbling up.

Do: Pickleball or tennis, then the library's rooftop views.

Echo Park Lake

Free

Echo Park · 751 Echo Park Ave

A palm-ringed lake with swan pedal boats, a fountain and a walking loop. The lotus bed blooms each summer.

Do: Rent a swan boat at sunset.

Los Feliz · Vermont Ave entrance

More than 4,000 acres of trails, canyons and picnic lawns right behind the neighborhood.

Lake Balboa Park

Free

Van Nuys · 6300 Balboa Blvd
