# The best parks in Los Angeles

31 picks across the neighborhoods I cover, grouped by area. Each one is written from what critics and real people consistently say about it.

### Coast

### Malibu Creek State Park

$

[Malibu](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/malibu/#eat) · 1925 Las Virgenes Rd

Canyon trails, a rock pool for swimming and the old M*A*S*H filming site.

**Do:** Hike to the M*A*S*H site, about 2.5 miles each way.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+Creek+State+Park+1925+Las+Virgenes+Rd+Malibu+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Malibu+CA)

### Will Rogers State Historic Park

$

[Pacific Palisades](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/pacific-palisades/#eat) · 1501 Will Rogers State Park Rd

Will Rogers' old ranch, with a polo field, horses and the Inspiration Point trail's big ocean and city views. Some areas are still being repaired.

**Do:** Hike the loop to Inspiration Point.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Will+Rogers+State+Historic+Park+1501+Will+Rogers+State+Park+Rd+Pacific+Palisades+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pacific+Palisades+CA)

### Ballona Wetlands Ecological Reserve

Free

[Playa Vista](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/playa-vista/#eat) · Ballona Wetlands

A protected coastal wetland with birding and a walking and bike path that links Marina del Rey and Playa del Rey.

**Do:** An early walk or bike ride along the path. Parts may be closed for restoration.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ballona+Wetlands+Ecological+Reserve+Ballona+Wetlands+Playa+Vista+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Vista+CA)

### Palisades Park

Free

[Santa Monica](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/santa-monica/#eat) · Ocean Ave

The bluff-top park with views down the coast and across to the Santa Monica Mountains.

**Do:** Walk the north end to skip the pier crowds.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palisades+Park+Ocean+Ave+Santa+Monica+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-santa-monica)

### Venice Canals

Free

[Venice](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/venice/#eat) · Off Dell Ave

Four blocks of canal houses and arched footbridges, and the calm counterpoint to the boardwalk.

**Do:** Walk the loop. You can launch your own non-motorized boat from the Venice Blvd ramp.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Venice+Canals+Off+Dell+Ave+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california)

### Franklin Canyon Park

Free

[Beverly Hills Post Office](https://www.nathanielgrivas.com/neighborhoods/bhpo/#eat) · 2600 Franklin Canyon Dr
