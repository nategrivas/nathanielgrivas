# Westchester: where to eat, drink and go

Westchester sits on the bluffs just north of LAX, home to Loyola Marymount University and a set of neighborhood restaurants and coffee shops along Manchester Avenue, La Tijera Boulevard and the Westchester Triangle.

### Coffee

### The Boy & The Bear

$

7403 S La Tijera Blvd

A coffee roaster with a roomy cafe, good wifi and easy parking, popular for working.

**Order:** A flat white with a pastry.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Boy+%26+The+Bear+7403+S+La+Tijera+Blvd+Westchester+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westchester+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Breakfast

### The Coffee Company

$$

8751 S La Tijera Blvd

A busy corner cafe minutes from LAX with a long breakfast menu and house-made biscuits.

**Order:** An omelet with the Potatoes O'Brien and a biscuit.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Coffee+Company+8751+S+La+Tijera+Blvd+Westchester+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Google Maps reviews (local favorites we checked)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Westchester+Los+Angeles+CA)

### Dinner

### Ayara Thai

$$

6245 W 87th St

A family-owned Thai restaurant that has been a Westside favorite since 2004.

**Order:** Chicken satay, pad see ew and mango sticky rice. Book ahead; it fills up.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ayara+Thai+6245+W+87th+St+Westchester+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [OpenTable diner reviews](https://opentable.com.au/features/best-restaurants-for-foodies-lax-ca)

Lunch

6224 W Manchester Ave

### Date night

### Tomat

$$$

6261 W 87th St

A seasonal, garden-to-table restaurant and bakery with a rooftop patio that looks out on planes landing at LAX.

**Order:** Morning pastries, or dinner on the roof.
