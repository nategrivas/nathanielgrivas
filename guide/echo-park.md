# Echo Park: where to eat, drink and go

Echo Park wraps around its lake, with swan boats and a palm-lined loop, and Sunset Boulevard runs through it with some of the Eastside's best bars, taquerias and music rooms. Dodger Stadium sits right above it in Chavez Ravine.

### Coffee

### Eightfold Coffee

$

1294 Sunset Blvd

A bright, roomy cafe with free Wi-Fi, great croissants and no charge for alternative milks.

**Order:** A lavender latte and a savory pastry. Park up on Kensington and take the stairs down.

### Echo Park Lake

Free

A palm-ringed lake with swan pedal boats, a fountain and a walking loop. The lotus bed blooms each summer.

**Do:** Rent a swan boat at sunset.
