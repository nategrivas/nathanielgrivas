# Venice: where to eat, drink and go

Venice packs a beach town, one of LA's best restaurant streets and a handful of genuinely odd landmarks into a couple of square miles. Abbot Kinney and Rose Avenue carry most of the eating and drinking; the boardwalk, canals and skatepark carry the rest.

### Coffee

### Menotti's Coffee Stop

$

56 Windward Ave

A tiny espresso bar a few steps off the boardwalk. People describe it as a friendly escape from the crowds, and the weekend line regularly spills onto the sidewalk.

**Order:** Any espresso drink to go before a walk on the boardwalk.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Menotti%27s+Coffee+Stop+56+Windward+Ave+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/restaurants/the-best-coffee-shops-in-venice) [Hotel Erwin](https://www.hotelerwin.com/venice-beach-coffee-shops/)

### Groundwork Coffee

$

671 Rose Ave

Organic, house-roasted coffee from one of LA's best-known local roasters. Discover LA and longtime regulars point to the Rose Avenue shop as the Venice location worth the trip.

**Order:** Drip coffee or a cortado.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Groundwork+Coffee+671+Rose+Ave+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/restaurants/the-best-coffee-shops-in-venice) [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/things-to-do-in-venice-california) [Foursquare](https://foursquare.com/top-places/venice-los-angeles/best-places-trendy)

### Intelligentsia Venice

$$

1331 Abbot Kinney Blvd

An airy, lab-like coffee bar that holds one of the highest Foursquare scores in Venice. Regulars rate the coffee and warn that it's busy and very much a scene.

**Order:** Pour-over, or an iced coffee for the walk down Abbot Kinney.

[Map ↗](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Intelligentsia+Venice+1331+Abbot+Kinney+Blvd+Venice+Los+Angeles+CA) Sources: [Foursquare](https://foursquare.com/top-places/venice-los-angeles/best-places-trendy) [Hotel Erwin](https://www.hotelerwin.com/venice-beach-coffee-shops/)

### Deus Ex Machina

$

1001 Venice Blvd

An espresso bar inside a custom motorcycle and surf shop. Visitors like browsing the bikes and boards while they wait.

**Order:** Espresso, then a look around the shop.

### Record king tides and big surf hit the coast on Thanksgiving morning

An extreme high tide of about 8 feet arrived Thursday morning, with a coastal flood advisory for the Malibu coast and LA County beaches through noon and a high surf warning until 9 p.m. Forecasters expect waves of roughly 6 to 11 feet with strong rip currents, and the LA Harbor tied its all-time high-water mark. If you're heading to the beach this weekend, check tide times before parking in beach lots and stay off the rocks and jetties.
