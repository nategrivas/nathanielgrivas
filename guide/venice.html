Venice: where to eat, drink and go

Venice packs a beach town, one of LA's best restaurant streets and a handful of genuinely odd landmarks into a couple of square miles. Abbot Kinney and Rose Avenue carry most of the eating and drinking; the boardwalk, canals and skatepark carry the rest.

A tiny espresso bar a few steps off the boardwalk. People describe it as a friendly escape from the crowds, and the weekend line regularly spills onto the sidewalk.

Order: Any espresso drink to go before a walk on the boardwalk.

Record king tides and big surf hit the coast on Thanksgiving morning

An extreme high tide of about 8 feet arrived Thursday morning, with a coastal flood advisory for the Malibu coast and LA County beaches through noon and a high surf warning until 9 p.m. Forecasters expect waves of roughly 6 to 11 feet with strong rip currents, and the LA Harbor tied its all-time high-water mark. If you're heading to the beach this weekend, check tide times before parking in beach lots and stay off the rocks and jetties.
